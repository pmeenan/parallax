To be completely candid: All three of those have significant baggage in the gaming and tech worlds. If you want a clean working title that won't cause immediate confusion or step on massive IPs, you will probably want to pivot slightly.

Here is the exact baggage you would be dealing with:

- Vanguard: Massive baggage. Call of Duty: Vanguard was a recent major AAA release. Furthermore, Riot Games (creators of League of Legends and Valorant) uses "Vanguard" as the name for their highly publicized, kernel-level anti-cheat software.

- Expanse: Heavy baggage. The Expanse is a universally beloved sci-fi television and book franchise, and it literally just had a major narrative game release (The Expanse: A Telltale Series) recently. Anyone who hears it will immediately assume it is tied to that IP.

- Hyperion: Medium-heavy baggage. In the gaming world, Hyperion is the famous mega-corporation in the Borderlands franchise (they make the guns and the robots). It is also heavily associated with Dan Simmons' legendary sci-fi book series, the Hyperion Cantos.
