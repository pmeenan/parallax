# District 1 greybox reference

Contract: D-090. This is the structural reference for the procedural M1 placeholder kit,
not a final-art reference sheet.

- Metric, Y-up 4,096 m square; 256 m cells.
- Central hilltop castle and moat remain the district-wide anchor.
- Village gives way to fields, then northern forest; the south edge is shoreline.
- Paths connect the castle, village, fields, forest, and shore.
- Castle, village, and forest each carry a future District 2 entrance marker.
- Materials use a bright, high-readability palette and must remain dynamically relightable.
- Distant mountains are vista metadata outside the playable surface.

The generator may vary placement inside these constraints only by advancing its recorded
version and decision. It may not import protected D&D names, designs, or rules text.
