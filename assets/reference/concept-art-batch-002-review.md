# Batch 002 — castle direction selection

**Human-selected castle: A, for its classic castle character and large tower.** All three alternatives passed
theme/consistency and quality/construction first-pass reviews with GPT-6 Astra / low,
plus lead inspection. These are generated concept proposals, not implemented captures.
Village B remains selected; this choice concerns the castle, not village alternatives A/C.

| A — round keep | B — low curtain fortress | C — stepped rectangular keep |

| ![A round keep](concepts/batch-002/dir-003-a-round-keep-v2.png) | ![B curtain fortress](concepts/batch-002/dir-003-b-curtain-fortress-v1.png) | ![C rectangular keep](concepts/batch-002/dir-003-c-rectangular-keep-v1.png) |
| Strong central landmark, closest to the castle presence in selected village B | Broad defended compound, restrained vertical presence | Monumental geometric silhouette with stacked masonry tiers |

The lead favors **A** for the strong landmark and relationship to village B. B is a
useful quieter fortress direction; C makes the castle more imposing and geometric.
The high viewpoint makes the complete moat visible but does not settle street-level
presence. All share a wide, rather regular water ring; its width and shoreline shape
are unmeasured composition choices and must not replace authored geography.

## A — round keep

![Round keep](concepts/batch-002/dir-003-a-round-keep-v2.png)

Dominant round keep with compact supporting defenses. The
[earlier lower view](concepts/batch-002/dir-003-a-round-keep-v1.png) is useful for
silhouette context, but failed the rear-moat visibility brief and is not loop evidence.
The high view supplies that missing relationship; its reconstructed hidden terrain
is interpretation, not surveyed layout.

## B — low curtain fortress

![Curtain fortress](concepts/batch-002/dir-003-b-curtain-fortress-v1.png)

Low stout walls and towers around subordinate service halls. Less vertical landmark
presence; the approach reads as an elevated ramp and needs a construction section.
Interior buildings are proposed castle service structures, not relocated village houses.

## C — stepped rectangular keep

![Rectangular keep](concepts/batch-002/dir-003-c-rectangular-keep-v1.png)

Strong geometric mass with lower wings and square towers. Interior stairs and gate
connections remain unresolved at this distance. Select its overall architectural
character, not every inferred opening or tier dimension.
