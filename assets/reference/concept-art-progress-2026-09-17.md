## Workflow correction and proposed finish planning

The program already says ordinarily6–12 images/sheets per human review. Two parallel
generation lanes were incorrectly coupled to two-image human handoffs. Starting with
the next packet, collect6–12 independently screened images (target8–12 when independent
coverage warrants it), organized in vertical labeled thematic groups. Keep at most2
generation lanes and both independent Astra-low screens/root before corrections or
dependent images. Stop earlier only for a real artistic dependency, exhausted bounded
allowance or review blocker; do not fabricate extra work merely to fill a packet.
Internal two-image work packages may retain their existing receipts/IDs; their
completion is not automatically a human-review pause. Keep individual pass/time
allowances and readable cumulative progress updates. No unattended full-catalog run.
Twelve images instead of two means sixfold fewer approval interruptions for equivalent
work, not sixfold faster generation. Independent review quality remains unchanged.

Before promising an end date, reconcile each remaining target into a finite completion
checklist: essential new concept outputs, necessary consistency repairs, technical/
motion evidence, and later-feature exploratory references. Do not automatically grow
new alternative/state sets after adequate direction is accepted. Existing required
coverage cannot be silently waived; moving grounded motion/production detail or the47
missing deferred-feature references out of this pre-production gate would be an
explicit D187/whole-catalog sequence change for human decision, not done by this audit.
No scope reduction, new image cap or milestone completion is claimed.

Known pending choice: Batch162A knack assignment has passed screens but lacks explicit
human approval; bring it forward in the next packet rather than lose it. Reshape open/
closed are approved. Batch164 both images approved and status updated with this audit.

## Human confirmation — 2026-09-17

The human accepts larger review packets. Aim for8–12, with smaller packets only
when artistic dependencies or bounded-work limits require them. Appearance, lighting
and motion candidates still receive human review; agent screening never substitutes
for acceptance. This clarifies packet scheduling within the existing6–12 range,
not a delegation of artistic authority.

The human is open to deciding whether motion artwork is needed per effect, or
whether motion should be reviewed with generated models/effects. No blanket motion
art waiver, deferred-feature exclusion or D187 sequence change is authorized yet.
Recommended evaluation: keep concepts where silhouette, visual language, onset/impact
shape or an artistic alternative is unresolved; assess timing, weight, contact and
physics with animated previews/grounded references, preserving human approval.
This proposal does not claim that static art can validate those behaviors.

## Motion amendment accepted — D-192

The human agreed to the motion change on2026-09-17. The catalog no longer requires
motion artwork for every behavior before models/effects can be produced. Assess
artwork need per effect; otherwise record the intended behavior and carry a human-
reviewed animated evidence obligation into that subject's production brief. Motion
quality, grounded physical evidence and human acceptance are not waived. Other
appearance coverage, including future-feature reference scope, remains unchanged.
The118 Missing count above is the historical inventory before this amendment, not
118 mandatory future images. No target is marked completed merely by rescheduling
its motion review; a finite per-target disposition list remains necessary.
