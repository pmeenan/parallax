# Batch092 — stone and timber stairs

A v1 and B v6 pass independent quality/theme reviews (GPT-6 Astra/low) and lead
inspection. A v1 and B v6 doorway correction both human-approved.
Complementary construction proposals.

## A — limestone steps and terrace

![Stone steps](concepts/batch-092/kit-012-stone-steps-v1.png)

Five broad steps with solid masonry support, a retaining cheek and clear terrace.
Coarse weathered riser faces retain the approved village limestone character.

## B — timber stair and guarded landing

![Timber stair](concepts/batch-092/kit-013-timber-stair-v6.png)

Five attached tread levels plus landing, joined rails, braced posts and partly buried
stone feet. Unsupported bottom plank and oversized first rise corrected. A closed
oak door now gives the landing a purpose; full doorway frame and original guards
retained. Open-state swing/clearance remains to be authored and verified.

Exact dimensions, loads and traversal/foot-placement profiles remain open; these
do not commit a new building, entrance or route.
