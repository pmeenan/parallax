# Batch 001 — village direction comparison

Three sunny candidates for **DIR-002**, generated concepts, not game captures.
**Human selection: B**, for its character and quality; lamps must be flame-powered,
never electric. A's plaster was not liked; C's pristine walls and gutter treatment
felt too clean and high-end for the village. The [catalog](concept-art-catalog.md),
[batch brief](concept-art-batch-001.md) and [exact prompts/provenance/reviews](targets/DIR-002.md)
retain the requirements and open gaps. Each original received two subagent inspections
before the next generation call. Current review setting: GPT-6 Astra / low.

## A — plaster-led

![A — plaster-led sunny village](concepts/batch-001/dir-002-a-sunny-v2.png)

Closest continuation of the anchor's plaster/oak language. Rich surface detail and
clear lane; plaster is more distressed than the anchor and the street visibly descends.
Passed first-pass review after correcting portrait framing. No exact layout claim.

## B — timber-led

![B — timber-led sunny village](concepts/batch-001/dir-002-b-sunny-v1.png)

Stronger expressed timber, braces and jettied upper floors; broad walking route.
A more northern-European architectural character, with weathered plaster and darker
eaves. Passed first-pass review; hidden joins still need construction sheets.

## C — limestone-led

![C — limestone-led sunny village](concepts/batch-001/dir-002-c-sunny-v2.png)

Substantial exposed limestone at the foreground lower storey, cleaner plaster above,
restrained timber. Revision resolves the first version's plaster dominance.
Passed both first-pass reviews after correction; the farther houses remain a mixed kit.

## Selection and remaining work

Use B as the village direction, preserving its weathered character. Show candle/oil
flame construction in lamp details; exclude electric fixtures and C-style gutters or
pristine finishes. Castle identities and apparent distances vary across these
independent alternatives; none selects a castle design or relocates authored geography.
The anchor still governs overall quality.

Next, finish B's matched gloomy reference coverage and useful material/detail
views. A's rejected overcast attempts are not accepted substitutes. Then proceed to
the catalog's castle/village relationship and underground direction targets before
dependent production sheets. Rights review, exact geometry/scale, moving-camera
stability, the rest of the catalog and all runtime acceptance remain open.
