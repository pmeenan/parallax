## Coverage and sequence

waterfront to the village core and inland castle, with a separate eastern beach

Populate inhabited studies with the original fantasy world's Humans, Skarn and
Wickfolk, and wizard/aetherworker, knight and rogue silhouettes as well as working
sailors and merchants. Avoid human-only historical reenactment; visual roles do not
add gameplay classes or lock unselected folk anatomy.

Hospitality direction: [Batch 010 B courtyard](concept-art-batch-010-review.md) is
human-selected for story possibilities and chance encounters. Preserve its open

This complements overall port A; it does not shrink the port or select folk anatomy.

Working-waterfront direction: [Batch 011 B](concept-art-batch-011-review.md) is selected

Merchant direction: [Batch 012 A and B](concept-art-batch-012-review.md) are both
human-selected. Include dedicated waterfront counting houses near ships/cargo and a
shared merchant courtyard for trade meetings and chance encounters. They coexist;
do not collapse the selection to one office arrangement.

Tavern interior: [Batch 013 B connected rooms](concept-art-batch-013-review.md) is

Preserve partially separated seating, discreet conversation pockets and public/private
contrast while keeping readable access. No particular faction or quest is implied.

Inn arrival: [Batch 014 A](concept-art-batch-014-review.md) is human-selected because

and lodging service. Distinct tavern/inn spaces may share hospitality operations;
do not impose a separate modern hotel reception role. No new named NPC is committed.

Guest accommodation: [Batch 015 A private chamber](concept-art-batch-015-review.md)

Corridor: [Batch 016 B bend](concept-art-batch-016-review.md) is human-selected for

## Gates and unresolved questions

Latest timber-berth direction: moor ship close alongside with rope fenders and
visible mooring lines; use a short boarding ramp. Keep the same elevated perspective
and distinct pier/hull, with a narrow fender-filled seam rather than a large water
gap. Gangway must accommodate relative movement; exact joint/tide validation open.

Cargo-handling correction: a fixed frame over paving does not satisfy ship loading.
Show a useful transfer path from ship cargo access to a quay landing area, along
with plausible supports/reach and separate pedestrian space. Screen the work the
equipment performs before treating material/connection quality as sufficient.
The hand-powered loading design must also show useful mechanical advantage and a
load-holding mechanism. Batch 022 uses an explicit assumed-load/hand-effort estimate;
an attractive pulley alone is insufficient and the estimate is not a load rating.
Batch 022 v2 passes overall direction screening; Batch 023 clarifies hand crank.
Brake/auxiliary wheel and tackle continuity remain explicit mechanical blockers.
Do not mistake readable control placement for a validated hand-powered load path.
Batch 023 visual direction is human-accepted; its mechanical blockers remain open.

All are planned visual targets, not commitments to expand current milestone gameplay.
Use actual district descriptor and shoreline surveys for placement before implementation.
Large hulls need sufficient berth depth, entrance/turning clearance and tide range;
none can be established from blue water in a concept. Record proposed dimensions until
grounded. Breakwaters, dredging and new basins need deliberate design/implementation
approval rather than silently altering geography for an attractive picture.

Human correction (2026-09-06): Batch 008 was substantially underscaled. Overall
illustrations must communicate a **real port district**: multiple long substantial piers,
several large ships and a developed waterfront street network with cargo yards,
warehouses, merchant offices, tavern and inn. Do not reuse the small fishing village
composition as a limit on port size. Preserve grounded practical construction rather
than luxury styling. ENV-033/034 cover the hospitality and merchant-office district.
The castle remains inland; a supply route can be shown leaving frame without inventing
its location. Retain original weathered wood/stone/iron/rope, flame-only lighting and
AAA photoreal quality. Human artistic selection, subagent reviews, provenance/rights
and full daylight/night/weather profiles apply as in the main concept program.
