# Batch 009 — substantial port district

Both revised concepts passed two GPT-6 Astra / low subagent screens for substantial
port scale, medieval theme and composition quality. The human selected A for its
greater scale and capacity for interesting scenarios/interactions. B feels too small
and village-like for this port; it is not selected.
Earlier Batch 008 options remain rejected for insufficient scale.

## A — dense waterfront

![A — dense waterfront](concepts/batch-009/env-028-a-port-v1.png)

Large waterfront premises and continuous quay frontage create a dense, established
port. Multiple working piers and large ships serve several blocks inland.

Proposed building key: the large arched three-storey building in the upper-left
quarter is the inn; the lower gabled building immediately to its right, facing the
small square, is the tavern; narrow fronts behind the upper quay are merchant offices.
Large foreground quay buildings are warehouses. Uses need dedicated close views.

## B — open working yards and social square

![B — open working yards and social square](concepts/batch-009/env-028-b-port-v1.png)

Long piers connect to broad cargo aprons, with warehouses separating loading from
the inhabited streets. More room for dockside interactions and varied activity.

Proposed building key: left-hand porch and outdoor tables mark the tavern; the tall
arched stone building behind it is the inn; the narrow frontages across the rear of
the square are merchant offices. The waterfront timber sheds are warehouses.

A is the selected scale and dense architectural direction. Preserve that developed
port character in close views; building functions remain proposals
until ENV-033/034 close views, and ship types/rigs remain KIT-035/036 work.

Both require berth/depth, maneuvering, gangway and structural validation. The accepted
small beach dock remains a separate reference outside these views; neither image
proves its eventual map relationship. Actual castle supply connection stays open.
