# Batch 076 — castle approach and moat bank

A v2 and corrected B v4 are human-approved. B v2's pair-continuity pass was
withdrawn after human feedback: missing waterside house terrace and obscured bridge
junction. B v4 also corrects the house heights missed in v3: full upper walls and
roofs now project above street. Both independent reviews and lead pass this repair.
Complementary views, not mutually exclusive alternatives.

## A — southwest gate approach

![Southwest gate approach](concepts/batch-076/env-012-southwest-gate-v2.png)

Inland view with direct bridge entry into the tall curtain-wall gateway. Physical
rest marker and bench sit off the foreground approach. Replaces the old detached
lower-gate direction.

## B — moat-bank street and crossing

![Moat-bank street](concepts/batch-076/env-013-moat-bank-v4.png)

Waterside houses occupy the lower terrace but rise above street, matching A's
visible upper walls/roofs rather than the sunken roof treatment in B v3.
Open cobbled apron makes the turn onto bridge clear; bank wall ends beside entry.
Developed dry bank, supported arched crossing and gateway seen from north of bridge.
Small tower/window details vary; a shared blockout must settle exact geometry,
bridge grade/clearance, abutments and continuous cart routes.

V1s retained as rejected lineage: A had sea behind the inland castle, B had the
wrong-side camera, and both varied the gateway. [Prompts](concept-art-batch-076.md)
and [provenance/reviews](targets/ENV-012-013-gate-moat.md).
