# Batch115 — limestone palette candidates

Both v3 candidates human-approved. Buff and gray are proposed palette options beside the
approved cream baseline; no district assignment or courtyard replacement is implied.

First-pass A1 and B1 passed theme screening but failed quality screening: the stone
and surrounding earth acquired softened, looping detail. Lead inspection agreed.
Both lanes therefore used the original Batch108 cream slab again for their second
pass, without using either rejected image as input. A2 and B2 also failed both
quality and theme screening: bright outlined loops and softened relief remained
visible on the stone. Lead inspection agreed. Neither v1 nor v2 is accepted.

A named one-pass / 15-minute extension per lane now uses the original as a
style/family reference for fresh comparable palette studies, rather than requiring
an exact recolor. The natural granular surface requirement remains unchanged.
Third-pass A3 and B3 pass both independent reviews and lead inspection. Sharper,
irregular granular detail replaces the softened looping appearance. Pores and pale
inclusions remain dense; these are appearance studies, not calibrated material maps.
