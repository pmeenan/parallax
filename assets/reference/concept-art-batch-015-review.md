# Batch 015 — inn guest accommodation

The selected open hall combines barkeep and lodging management. A private chamber
is human-selected for locked doors, private conversations, stealing and adventure
opportunities. B remains an unselected shared-room alternative for this use. Exact
floorplans, lock construction and gameplay implementation remain open.

## A — private traveler chamber

![A — private guest room](concepts/batch-015/env-033-guest-a-private-v1.png)

One bed, writing space, travel chest and washing provisions give a traveler privacy
and a quiet place to plan. Worn but cared-for furnishings match the inn's character.

## B — shared travelers room

![B — shared guest room](concepts/batch-015/env-033-guest-b-shared-v1.png)

Separate beds and individual baggage storage line a common aisle. Mixed fantasy
travelers can exchange stories while settling in. Five full/partial beds appear,
including one cropped in the foreground; capacity is not a fixed design contract.

These are proposed interiors, not metric layouts or final character models. Furniture
clearances, bed access and species/folk proportions require later spatial references.
Both passed GPT-6 Astra / low theme and quality reviews on their first generation.
In A, washware occupies the spare chair; in B, a loose boot's relationship to the
Skarn's legs is ambiguous. These are future prop details, not room-layout blockers.
