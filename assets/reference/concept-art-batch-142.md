# Batch142 — ordinary daylight sky baseline

LIGHT-020 P1. Two fresh photographic sky-only views, complementary clear-weather
and full overcast. No land, sea, architecture, mountain silhouette, horizon objects,
people, birds or text; these are sky appearance studies, not new geography or full
world lighting replacements. Look upward at broad natural sky, modest wide view
without fisheye distortion. Ordinary coastal-temperate atmosphere, no fantasy nebula.

A clear daylight: natural blue sky covering most frame, a few scattered small
fair-weather cumulus clouds with realistic soft volume, irregular flat-ish bases,
sunlit white tops and gentle gray undersides. Modest thin distant wisps optional.
Keep blue space dominant; no giant cotton castles, dramatic sunset, sunrays or
oversaturated cyan. Sun outside frame, natural gradient, no visible moon/stars.

B full overcast: continuous broad pale-gray low cloud sheet with softly uneven
layering and gentle larger-scale tonal variation. No blue holes, visible sun disk,
beams, storm funnel, dramatic anvil or harsh black masses. Calm readable ordinary
gray day, not featureless flat fill nor intricate painterly embossed billows.

Both natural camera-like cloud softness, believable water-vapor scattering,
no repeat tiles, crunchy microcontrast, painted swirls or artificial volumetric
sculptures. Photographic reference aesthetic matching grounded project. Clear sky
supports direct sun in world; overcast supports broad diffuse illumination, but
these stills do not establish radiance, exposure, solar schedule or cloud motion.

Reuse approved060 sunny/overcast and061 dawn/dusk as scene-level companions.
Storm/night/sun/moon/star positions and mountain horizon integration remain open;
volumetric implementation remains separate LIGHT-012 deferred research.

Two lanes, max two passes/30 active minutes each. Fresh text-only builtin imagegen;
save exact prompt before call, retain native source/output equal SHA256 bytes/dims
UTC/tooltime. Model/seed unavailable, rights pending public shipping, no runtime
admission. Independent Astra/low quality/theme plus root before revisions; human
appearance approval closes each view. No invented calibrated sky parameters.
