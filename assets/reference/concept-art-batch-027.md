## Exact A prompt — Burrow-gnawer

```text
Create a single AAA photoreal movie-quality original medieval fantasy creature concept for Project Parallax. Supplied courtyard establishes tactile realism and daylight quality only; do not reproduce its architecture. BURROW-GNAWER, a small field vermin that lives in burrows, bites in small swarms and flees when its pack is depleted. One whole creature only, standing naturally in a three-quarter side view beside a modest burrow in an earthen field bank, all four feet and entire tail clearly visible, sparse grass and loose roots, simple soft-focus farmland backdrop. Proposed biology: compact low quadruped roughly badger-sized, coarse earth-brown fur, powerful digging forelimbs, short blunt claws, rounded low ears, blunt muscular rodent-like muzzle with a pair of visible gnawing incisors, small dark alert eyes, short thick tapering lightly furred tail. Integrate these into ONE coherent living mammal with believable joints, weight, jaw and fur, not an assembled chimera. Read as hardy troublesome vermin rather than cute pet, giant boss, undead or monster with armor. Mouth slightly open enough to show bite tools, no blood or injury. Natural paws, no human hands, no duplicated limbs, no oversized teeth or fantasy spikes. Detailed dusty fur, whiskers, moist nose, earth on claws, sunlight with soft shadows, sharp creature silhouette and enough clear ground to understand its stance. Original restrained fantasy wildlife, believable real animal cinematography, not cartoon or painterly. No labels, text, UI or measurement marks. Size is proposal only; no new named powers or abilities.
```

## Exact B prompt — Greymaw

```text
Create a single AAA photoreal movie-quality original medieval fantasy creature concept for Project Parallax. Supplied courtyard establishes tactile realism and daylight quality only; do not reproduce its architecture. GREYMAW, a forest pack predator that circles, flanks, lunges and pounces. One whole animal only, alert standing in a three-quarter side view at a sunlit woodland edge, all four feet and entire tail visible, uncluttered leaf litter and natural forest background out of focus. Proposed biology: large agile wolf-scale quadruped with muscular shoulders, deep chest, flexible lean waist and powerful hindquarters; coarse charcoal-gray coat shading to ash-gray muzzle and throat, short rounded-triangular ears, broad predatory muzzle and amber eyes, long balancing furred tail. Coherent original mammalian anatomy, natural digitigrade legs and articulated paws, body built for a low stalking approach and a forceful pounce. Quiet watchful threat, mouth only slightly open showing ordinary predatory teeth, no blood. Subtle distinctive dark saddle and pale weathered-looking muzzle, no magical glow, armor, horns, tentacles or exaggerated saber fangs. Avoid simply enlarged housecat or cartoon wolf; real animal weight, gravity, fur direction, believable joints and musculature. Natural daylight under trees, detailed fur/whiskers/nose, movie-quality photorealism. No humanoids, no labels, writing, UI or measurements. Anatomy and size are proposals, not new powers or an extra bestiary archetype.
```
