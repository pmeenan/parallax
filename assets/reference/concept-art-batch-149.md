# Batch149 — interaction marker appearance

UI-003 P1 two complementary static proposals, not competing alternatives. A close
NPC interaction; B visible distant waystone. No runtime changes. Dark translucent
charcoal, restrained bronze outline and ivory text from selected145 B. Markers are
screen UI, not physical luminous signs. Keep world readable and realistic.

A: edit approved146 ready screenshot; preserve Mara, setting, camera and vitals.
Remove entire conversation panel and all its controls. Small unobtrusive ivory
conversation-bubble glyph above/beside shoulder without covering face. A compact
lower-center prompt only: Mara Venn on first line, E · Talk on second. No reply,
quest punctuation, new abilities or full ability bar. Isolated interaction-overlay
study, not specification to hide the normal exploration HUD.

B: approved091 village waystone as setting/material input and145 B as UI style
only. Show the same recognizable irregular gray stone, recessed branching gold
channels, nearby flowers and tree seating from several paces farther back. Stone
fully visible, relatively small in middle distance; keep background photographic.
Compact ivory diamond and small Waystone label immediately above it, modest charcoal
backing/bronze edge. No E prompt outside interaction range, no distance number,
teleport, portal, route arrow or extra map/HUD. No new geography authority. This
illustrates reduced distant marker prominence, not a metric range threshold.

Both are proposed visible-target states; occluded markers, nodes, entrances and
discovery remain open. Stills do not establish occlusion, range logic, motion,
accessibility or final glyphs. Two independent generation lanes, maximum two passes
and30 active minutes each. Save exact prompt before builtin imagegen; actually view
all references; native-copy receipt with equalSHA bytes/dimensions UTC/tooltime,
reference roles/hashes, unavailable model/seed and rights pending public shipping.
No external imagery, runtime/library admission or commits. Both independent Astra
low screens plus root actual view before revision or dependent generation; human
artistic approval to close.
