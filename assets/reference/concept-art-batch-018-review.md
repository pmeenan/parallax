# Batch 018 — dockside conversations

Supply-yard A is selected for substantial working-port character without fortress
treatment. These alternatives explore where sailors, captains and travelers meet.
Both are human-selected as different examples of conversation spaces; they coexist.

## A — open quay meeting pocket

![A — open quay meeting pocket](concepts/batch-018/env-032-a-quay-v1.png)

An open place beside the ships for news, introductions and chance encounters.
The broad waterfront stays visible behind the conversation, with room to approach.

## B — sheltered warehouse recess

![B — sheltered warehouse recess](concepts/batch-018/env-032-b-recess-v1.png)

A bench and barrel table under a warehouse awning provide a more settled,
sheltered conversation while the working waterfront remains immediately outside.

Both passed theme and construction screening (GPT-6 Astra, low thinking) and lead
review. A's water setback and cargo separation still need metric layout validation.
