# Batch 025 — Human, Skarn and Wickfolk

Human-selected: B, because it reads more realistic. Height remains a separate choice.

Two proposed folk directions. Left to right: adult Human knight, Skarn aetherworker,
Wickfolk rogue. These example loadouts are available across folk in the classless
game; anatomy, clothing and role are separate choices. Features may be combined.

## A — softer facial features

![A: Human, Skarn and Wickfolk](concepts/batch-025/dir-006-a-folk-v1.png)

More naturalistic Skarn face with visible mineral skin texture; compact framed
crystal catalyst. Wickfolk has understated pointed ears and a mature adult face.

## B — stronger fantasy features

![B: Human, Skarn and Wickfolk](concepts/batch-025/dir-006-b-folk-v1.png)

Heavier Skarn brow with gold mineral markings, sharper Wickfolk ears and facial
silhouette. Catalyst reads closer to an alchemical vessel. The stronger features
remain proposals, not a new creature type or canonical racial identity.

Both retain photoreal weathered materials. Wickfolk rendered around four-fifths
Human height in both, rather than the proposed two-thirds; height is a separate
open choice. Clothing is uniformly dark and distressed; later variants need broader
everyday condition/value coverage. Full anatomy, expressions and equipment assembly
require dedicated sheets after direction selection.
