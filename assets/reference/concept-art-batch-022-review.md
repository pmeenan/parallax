# Fishing vessel and revised ship-loading crane

The fixed cargo hoist was rejected because it could not transfer cargo between
ship and quay. The replacement shows the intended transfer directly.

## Large fishing vessel

![Fishing vessel](concepts/batch-021/kit-036-fishing-v1.png)

Single mast, open net-working deck and modest shelter; distinct from the merchant
ship and small beach boats. Both theme and quality screens passed this direction.
Background skyline is incidental, not a new castle/map-placement decision.

## Ship-to-quay crane

![Revised quay crane](concepts/batch-022/kit-037-quay-crane-v2.png)

Jib reaches the open hold, with a shore landing bay, geared hand winch and multi-part
tackle. Both reviewers passed the visual direction; mechanical acceptance remains open.

The proposed 500 kg gross-load calculation uses a 4:1 tackle, 4:1 geared drum,
0.45 m crank radius, maximum wound drum radius 0.12 m and assumed 60% efficiency:
about **136 N (14 kg-force)** at the handle and **21 crank turns per metre lifted**.
This estimate was independently checked; it is not a rated load capacity. The
mechanical-advantage basis is described by [OpenStax](https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/9-3-simple-machines).

The painting does **not** establish exact rope/gear ratios or show a clear holding
brake. The platform brace also needs a detail view establishing rotation clearance.
Those remain required mechanism studies, alongside structural/berth validation.
