# Batch138 — coastal water baseline

WATER-001 P2, static appearance only. Two complementary fresh photographic close
views of ordinary sea surface by the game's coast: A calm with small wavelets,
B light wind chop. No input image edits after137's repeated surface artifacts;
text-only generation, no claim of exact matched camera/shore geometry. No maps,
buildings, ships, docks or shore landmarks. Camera looks obliquely down from low
shore eye level across water only; no horizon required. The crop is intentionally
a material/appearance study rather than a new location. No numbered scales or UI.

Both natural muted blue-gray coastal water, subtle green in shallower near area,
ordinary dry overcast daylight reflected as soft broken light, darker troughs,
clear uneven organic wavelet directions and perspective scale diminishing away.
Photographic real water, no embossed contour loops, engraved patterns, gelatin,
flat decorative texture, plasticky highlights, tiled ripples or excessive bloom.
A: low-amplitude irregular ripples, mostly unbroken surface, no whitecaps.
B: visibly larger short wind-driven chop with irregular crests, only a few tiny
white foam breaks, still modest everyday conditions not storm/surf or giant swell.
Keep distinct amplitude/readability without inventing calibrated heights or forces.

No underwater scene, swimming mechanics, buoyancy proof or simulation technique.
Appearance approval leaves calm/chop temporal profiles, storm, foam detail, shore
interaction, scale/camera matching, reflections and physics open.

Two lanes max two passes/30 active minutes each. Save exact prompts BEFORE builtin
imagegen, native copies/full source and output hashes/bytes/dims/UTC/tooltime.
Text specifications only, no external images. Seed/model unavailable, rights/terms
pending public shipping; no runtime admission. Independent Astra/low quality and
theme screens plus root actual view before revision; human approval closes views.
