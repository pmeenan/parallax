# Batch116 — red limestone and courtyard joint plants

Both human-approved. A3 red limestone and B1 joint plants pass both independent
reviews and lead inspection.

## A — red limestone v3

![Red limestone](concepts/batch-116/mat-003-red-limestone-v3.png)

Restrained dusty rose-brown with broad worn planes and fine grain. Pores are subtle;
mottling and small fissures carry most weathering. Optional palette proposal,
not a replacement for the cream courtyard. Fresh composition, not a matched recolor.

## B — courtyard joint plants v1

![Courtyard joint plants](concepts/batch-116/veg-001-courtyard-joint-plants-v1.png)

Low broadleaf plants rooted in recessed soil. The receding tufts follow one joint
at fairly regular intervals; use this as leaf/contact detail, not a planting layout.

## Review history

A1 quality FAIL: restrained dusty rose works, but dense pale rings across the
central/front face look uniformly embossed. Lead inspection agrees the surface
needs calmer connected worn areas. Theme PASS: matte dusty rose remains mineral,
with dense pale outlines a residual. Lead retains the quality rejection and has
authorized the second allowed pass to calm the ring network without plastic smoothness.

A2 failed quality, theme and lead: loops still cover nearly the whole slab and
ground texture became more embossed. A fresh compact theme reviewer independently
confirmed the slab failure. A named one-pass / 15-minute extension now generates
from the written family brief without attaching the noisy raster. A3 quality PASS,
theme PASS and lead PASS: broad worn planes and granular material replace the
embossed ring network. Mottling/fissures remain visible; pores are subtle.

B1 quality PASS: broad leaves connect to crowns rooted below paver edges, with
most faces clear. Receding plants are somewhat evenly spaced along one diagonal
joint; record this as a layout limitation, not a requirement for planting repetition.
Lead inspection agrees the leaf/soil contact is readable. Theme PASS: low broadleaf
tufts emerge from soil, keeping most stone bare; modest leaf overlap at edges and
regular spacing remain residuals. B1 passes both reviews and lead inspection.

Independent quality and theme reviewers use GPT-6 Astra / low and inspect actual
images; lead adjudication is separate. No runtime or library acceptance.
