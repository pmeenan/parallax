# Batch176 — illustrated ability references

[Brief](concept-art-batch-176.md), [target coverage](targets/UI-008-ability-cards-176.md). All eight final cards human-approved. Final candidates are A/B/C/E/F/H v1 and D/G v2. Both independent GPT-6 Astra low reviewers quality158/theme158 and root actual views pass the final packet for scoped UI use. Quality review explicitly withholds exact botanical-brief compliance for H; root narrows H to generic gathering symbolism as recorded in the brief. G2 and final-set layout/readability passed both reviewers.

- A/B: Straight thrust/arrow symbols, exact existing rules. Symbolic icons do not prove weapon construction, reach, draw or contact. B adds a small bronze divider below the cost; nonblocking ornament variation within shared regions.
- C/E: Ember and pale violet force-wave palettes follow approved spell references, without new spell mechanics.
- D1 rejected for omitting 15 Aether. D2 restores the cost. Background texture is softer and description slightly higher than peers, but title/icon/type/cost/description/footer regions remain consistent and legible.
- F: Thick warm gold/ivory woven strands retain approved Mendweave direction; exact healing and suppression text.
- G1 rejected for adding a physical lantern, incorrectly making the light appear equipment-dependent. G2 removes it and lowers the empty arm; thin self-emitted warm ward contour supplies local light. No giant forcefield or invulnerability claim.
- H: Generic gathering leaf/ore symbol with restrained shimmer. Its lobed leaf does not match Bittergreen; accepted for generic icon screening only, not a replacement botanical design. Original042 remains botanical authority. Knack cost region stays blank.

Both screens and root adjudication preceded D/G revisions. Two passes for D/G, one for the other six; no further revision authorized in this packet. The shared175E2 layout was human-approved and screened before dependent generation. Final set retains common panel, title, icon, type, cost, description and footer regions. Costs and text checked against existing game-design/balance rules; 45ticks=.75s,360ticks=6s,1200ticks=20s.
