# Batch 017 — port supply yard and inland departure

Corridor B is selected for extra hall privacy. These next alternatives return to the
larger port: supplies move from the waterfront toward the existing village/castle.
A is human-selected: still a port, without becoming a fortress itself. The full
route remains to be designed; B is retained as an unselected alternative.

## A — open staging yard

![A — open staging yard](concepts/batch-017/env-031-a-yard-v1.png)

A broad yard flows into an open street between warehouse blocks. Long sightlines
make arrivals, cargo movement and chance encounters visible; tally stations and
side bays offer places to stop without closing the main route.

## B — gatehouse departure court

![B — gatehouse departure court](concepts/batch-017/env-031-b-gate-v1.png)

A substantial arch gives the supply route a distinct departure threshold. Cargo
yards and sheltered desks frame the approach, creating a focus for escorts,
conversations and departures while retaining the scale of the working port.

## Screening

Both passed independent theme and construction reviews (GPT-6 Astra, low thinking)
and lead visual review, with no correction required. Exact clearances, character
anatomy and the inland route connection remain open. Neither proposal implements
transport, customs or quest systems.
