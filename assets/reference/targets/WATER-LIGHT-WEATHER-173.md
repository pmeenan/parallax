# Water, rain and lighting appearance studies

[Batch173 brief](../concept-art-batch-173.md) defines eight independent deferred research studies. All eight candidates are human-approved; see the [review record](../concept-art-batch-173-review.md). No new authored lake, room, equipment, swimming mechanic or rendering-technique mandate; rights pending before public shipping. No library admission.

| Target | Image | Coverage and remaining production obligation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WATER-004 | A lake | Shore/shallows/deep-water appearance. Camera/weather variants, calibrated depth and moving water remain open. |
| LIGHT-010 | B indirect | Warm sunlit alcove and shaded recess. Cloth-specific bounce is subtle and not isolated from direct light; controlled source/occluder comparisons and leakage tests required. |
| WATER-008 | C underwater | Fully submerged camera, attenuation, surface above and caustic appearance. No swim mechanic. Motion, exposure transition and surface crossing remain open. |
| LIGHT-011 | D overlap | Two-flame baseline with distinct pools and grounded subjects. Dense many-light scene, moving sources and shadow continuity remain open. |
| WATER-006 | E splash | Small impact crown/cavity/droplet/ripple appearance. Source already submerged. Source size/speed, impact timing, collision and settling need grounded animated reference and human review. |
| LIGHT-016 | F focus | Optional photographic focus with recognizable background. Anonymous pouch is not canonical item replacement. Gameplay blur is not prescribed; moving-focus stability and legibility remain open. |
| WEATHER-002 | G runoff | Roof-edge rain discharge and receiving stone channel. Soil/water impact variants and measured continuous drainage/volumes remain open. |
| LIGHT-018 | H layering | Glass/leaf overlap, moss and separate background smoke. Smoke does not overlap bottle. Dense multilayer sorting, decal edges, moving-camera disocclusion and smoke/glass overlap remain open. |

All are pictorial camera/scale/light studies, not calibrated data. Later production briefs must cite the selected appearance and demonstrate moving-state behavior under D192 before human animation acceptance; these stills do not validate physical simulation or performance.
