## Human selection and binding direction

The human selected **B sunny v1** for the village's character and quality. Use that
original as the direction input for subsequent village concepts: expressed dark oak,
weathered plaster infill, terracotta roofs and limestone foundations/paving, with
credible everyday medieval construction. Do not clean up B toward C or replace its
plaster treatment with A's. Earlier agent comments about B's distressed plaster are
selection tradeoffs, not a mandate to remove the character the human selected.

**Village lamps are flame-powered only**, never electric. Resolve their construction
in detail references as candle or oil-flame lanterns with a credible flame source,
ventilation and mounting; no bulbs, electrical sockets, cables or LED-like emitters.
The sunny concept does not expose enough internal detail to certify a fuel mechanism.
Carry this constraint into prompts and both subagent review briefs. Ordinary village
lamps do not become magical or electric lights by inference.

A is not selected: its plaster appearance does not meet the human's quality preference.
C is not selected: its pristine walls, high-end finish and gutter/downpipe treatment
feel inappropriate for this village. Avoid that polished appearance and C-style
gutters/downpipes in subsequent village art. This is project art direction, not a
claim about what drainage technology existed historically.

The selection endorses B's artistic character and quality, not inferred dimensions,
castle design, authored world placement, rights clearance or runtime acceptance.
Batch 002 supplies agent-reviewed facade and candle-lantern detail drafts. B's matched
gloomy requirement remains open after two rejected attempts; reopen with a different
correspondence approach. A/C weather variants are no longer needed for direction
selection; the shared sunny/gloomy requirement still applies to B.
