# Batch 005 — entrance treatments

Arena B is selected for its "carved out of the earth" feel. These three entrance
treatments extend the selected village/castle/catacomb family. Each surface view is
followed by its underground companion. All six displayed images passed separate
GPT-6 Astra / low theme and quality reviews. They are context/material proposals,
not matched geometry or validated transition layouts.

## Castle undercroft

![Castle surface approach](concepts/batch-005/und-003-castle-surface-v1.png)

Practical doorway in castle masonry and bedrock; close inner wall hides the distant route.

![Castle underground arrival](concepts/batch-005/und-004-castle-arrival-v1.png)

Masonry landing merges with excavated passage. Left return can read as an alcove;
its route needs clearer treatment in blockout. [Prompts and review](targets/UND-003-004.md).

## Village well

![Village-well surface approach](concepts/batch-005/und-001-well-surface-v1.png)

Separate pedestrian stair beside the working well; no descent through the water shaft.
This treats the existing village-well transition, not the decorative courtyard well.

![Village-well underground arrival](concepts/batch-005/und-002-well-arrival-v1.png)

Readable return stair beside sealed masonry. Stair run/headroom and well rope mechanism
remain unresolved. [Prompts and review](targets/UND-001-002.md).

## Forest ruin

![Forest-ruin surface approach](concepts/batch-005/und-005-forest-surface-v1.png)

Small overgrown ruin with an intact arch and clear approach.

![Forest underground arrival](concepts/batch-005/und-006-forest-arrival-v2.png)

Single bent passage; the rejected first attempt looked like a fork. Return is behind
the camera. [Prompts and review](targets/UND-005-006.md).

The human accepted all three entrance treatments and their companion views; retain all.
These are three different authored entrances, not mutually exclusive alternatives. Dimensions,
hidden joins, door states, transition exposure/motion and full occlusion remain open.
Seven originals and hashes retained; no runtime changes. Changes remain uncommitted.
