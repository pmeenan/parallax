## Cycle 1 findings

The corrected native build completed in approximately four seconds. The retained
[source and direct images](master-2026-09-20/cycle1/author-note.md) measure
394.999 × 297.834 × 78.628 mm, 823,956 source triangles and zero nonmanifold edges.
The support plane meets the source's actual minimum height. These checks establish
source structure only, not visual or runtime acceptance.

Lead actual inspection of all five images rejects the visible construction: long
clean polygonal sides and a hard upper rim, isolated notch-like chips, stamped-looking
top depressions and excessively bright capture illumination. Thickness and broadly
squared mass are useful. Gray geometry is judged separately from deliberately absent
mineral color, soil, vegetation and fine material maps. The second cycle must improve
connected fracture/wear structure and capture legibility before materials can proceed.
Both independent Astra-low screens also return FAIL after actual multi-angle review.
They agree on long planar sides, a continuous shoulder and isolated scoop-like losses;
the requested revision varies shoulder heights and short fracture planes, clusters
chips, preserves calm top regions and makes the gray exposure readable.
