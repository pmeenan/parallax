# Leaf color candidate 2 — final independent screen

Inspected all seven actual images: matched close, close-opposing, close-unlit, walking and overview, plus transfer/native and transfer/reimport. Comparison is against the candidate1 and vegetation/candidate2 matched images inspected in the preceding screen. Scope remains color only.

**Disposition: retain candidate2 for human review. The within-leaf improvement is now clearly visible, and the representative transfer retains it.** This ends the two-cycle color study; no additional artistic cycle is requested.

The upright and front broad leaves now have legible soft chlorophyll pockets rather than nearly uniform fills. The left leaf reads warmer/lighter than the deeper right leaf. These differences persist in the unlit and opposing views. At walking distance the plant has a little more internal color structure; overview remains a set of small green accents, as expected at that distance. Most leaf area remains healthy green, and neither random speckling nor strong painted vein shadows dominates.

The change is restrained rather than a dramatic fresh/mature spectrum. Broad mottling is stronger than the evidence for age: partial edge fading remains quite subtle in the supplied close composition, and the colors alone do not identify an unambiguous leaf-age hierarchy. The large soft pockets can still read somewhat cloudy. Those are residual limitations to show the human, not grounds to erase the clear improvement or invent another cycle. Within the color-only scope this is preferable to both the first color candidate and the uniform baseline.

In the isolated transfer pair, corresponding darker pockets, yellow-green regions, sparse warm blemishes and vein color survive on all four leaves. I see no material color loss, brightness shift, silhouette regression or new visible artifact at the supplied image scale. This is a conditional pass for this representative specimen's visible transfer, not validation of every plant or runtime rendering.

Human artistic acceptance remains open. Existing source-map/triangle budget excesses are unaffected, so this screen is not full production QA. Shape, moss and approved paving/contact/lighting are deliberately outside this verdict.
