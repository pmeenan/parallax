# Vegetation candidate1: independent theme screen

Outcome: fails the natural-variety/reference-quality criterion at this first cycle. Secondary veins are a useful improvement, but geometry and distinct growth forms still need the remaining bounded revision before representative transfer.

Read vegetation-brief.md. Inspected close, close-opposing, close-unlit, close-gray, overview, walking, all four leaf maps, and the subsequently supplied grass-detail/moss-detail. Compared against previously inspected VEG-001 batch116. Gray retains normal relief and is not a geometry-only isolation.

- Broadleaf vein detail is now readable, restrained and responsive to opposing light. Color has subtle variation rather than random speckle. However, the close plant remains coarse angular panels with an overlapping upright paddle pair and broad flat lower leaves. Straight stems and abrupt leaf attachments reinforce a constructed appearance. VEG-001 has varied basal leaves, curved petioles, irregular cupping and less uniform outlines.
- Grass is a separate form in its close view, but its blades are very straight narrow spikes radiating from a common point. Uneven lengths alone do not supply natural bow, twist or splay.
- Moss is visibly a scattering of green polygon pebbles. It does not read as a living low cushion or fine clustered foliage. This is the clearest categorical failure.
- Most joints remain bare as requested. Nevertheless, walking/overview are still dominated by similar isolated upright leaf clumps; grass, moss and seedlings do not yet establish a convincing visible mix. Do not solve this by evenly filling joints or making every secondary form larger.
- Saved leaf-basecolor is almost black at direct inspection, unlike the native lit plant. Lead reports an export encoding mismatch with native packed color correct. Correct and recheck the file/transfer; do not interpret that report as artistic approval. Height/normal/roughness show a coherent but regular repeated vein fan.

One remaining cycle priorities: make moss an irregular connected low cushion with fine tuft/leaf texture rather than separate smooth pellets; use a few smoother asymmetric leaf contours and curved varied orientations, including small basal seedling leaves; bow/twist the grass blades and vary clump silhouette. Preserve the restrained vein scale, adding only subtle nonuniform branching/color character rather than oversized relief. Arrange different forms in a few uneven protected clusters so their coexistence is legible at walking scale while most joints remain bare.

Approved stone material, illumination and local soil contact are outside this verdict, as are unrelated paving seam/repetition issues. Source-only texture exceptions, representative export, production QA and human acceptance remain separate.
