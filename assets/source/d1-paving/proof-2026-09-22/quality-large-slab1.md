# Larger-slab candidate1 — independent construction screen

I inspected all six native renders against the large-slab brief and KIT-001. This is a diagnostic screen, not an artistic selection or full paving acceptance.

**Construction:** The 4 × 4 m field reads as substantially broader slabs than the 111-stone layout: 61 stones, including pieces up to 1.14 m across. Several joints now stop at larger faces, and the recorded longest grid seam falls from 16 to 14 cells. This is directionally closer to KIT-001's broad, interrupted paving, though thin rectangular strips and some long aligned runs still expose the underlying rectilinear kit. Around the larger pieces, a few T-junctions open into conspicuous earth wedges rather than the reference's consistently narrow packed seams.

**Contact and render:** Top, sunny, opposing, overcast, walking, and plant-contact views show a complete field with continuous earth and no obvious floating slab, stone intersection, bank-strip line, or lighting-specific tear. The close plant is seated in an earth joint, but deep shadow under its crown hides part of the stone/soil interface; these stills cannot prove triangle-level plant contact. The receipt reports no conservative stone AABB overlaps, 9.96 mm minimum AABB clearance, clear plant roots, and no low plant vertices inside conservative stone footprints. Its 94,240 ground triangles remain a source-study cost, not a production substrate result.

**Material scale:** Reusing three source stones at XY scales from 0.676 to 3.301 visibly magnifies pale mineral islands and surface grain on the largest slabs. In the walking view, the broad central faces have enlarged mottles and repeat recognizably beside smaller stones; this is a major limit on judging the final slab look. KIT-001's large stones have local surface detail and varied outlines without this source-scale jump. The 14-cell seam score also cannot resolve repeated mineral landmarks by itself.

**Evidence:** `layout.json` and the receipt agree on 61 stones and the 14-cell maximum; the layout file, three retained source files, and all recorded candidate outputs match their receipt SHA-256 values. These checks establish source/output identity, not render-geometry clearance or material acceptance. The scene contains provisional older plants and no accepted leaf-color/moss integration, so it cannot stand as the full approved vegetation/contact patch. Full QA, engine import, cost qualification and human artistic judgment remain open.
