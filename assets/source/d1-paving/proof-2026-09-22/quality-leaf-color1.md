# Leaf color cycle 1 screen

Verdict: partial native improvement, with a visible representative-transfer discrepancy. The color complaint is not yet convincingly resolved across the deliverable. One bounded color-only revision is justified after both screens; preserve all other channels and geometry.

Read leaf-color-brief.md. Inspected the actual baseline vegetation/candidate2/close.png against leaf-color/candidate1/close.png, then candidate close-opposing, close-unlit, walking and overview. Also inspected candidate1/transfer/native.png and reimport.png. This screen concerns color only; shape, moss and paving are outside scope.

The main rosette's front leaf is visibly warmer and lighter than before while the upper leaf is darker. The unlit view confirms this is more than changed illumination. Broad soft interveinal variation and small warm blemishes remain healthy and restrained, without harsh hue shifts, noisy speckling or exaggerated painted veins. However, the matched close change is modest: most leaves still occupy a narrow muted green range, and occasional faded margins are not clearly legible. Walking shows some separation, but does not strongly communicate lighter new growth versus darker mature leaves. Overview is too distant to substantiate fine color detail and should not drive extra contrast by itself.

The transfer pair does not retain the native between-leaf relationship. In native.png the upper leaf is darker and the front leaf conspicuously lighter/warmer; in reimport.png the upper leaf becomes lighter while the front leaf becomes darker, reducing or reversing that ordering. Within-leaf mottling, spots and veins remain visible, and the silhouette is consistent. This is a visible color-retention failure regardless of small whole-image pixel metrics. The mechanism is not established by this screen; verify the per-leaf color representation survives export rather than assuming the texture's presence proves it.

For the remaining bounded cycle, first preserve per-leaf color through the representative transfer. Then make growth-stage separation more legible in the matched close and walking views: a limited subset of lighter fresh leaves against deeper mature greens, with soft uneven interveinal patches and occasional localized faded margins. Keep most area healthy, preserve restrained blemish density, and avoid uniformly multiplying every leaf into the same new shade or amplifying all vein contrast. No change to normal, roughness, shape, lighting or unrelated ground is called for.

Source-only texture and vegetation budget limitations, full QA, rights and runtime/human acceptance remain separate and open. Native-only tint variation is insufficient for this brief; both within-leaf and between-leaf color need visible transfer retention.
