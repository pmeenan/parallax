# Vegetation cycle 2 final screen

Verdict: retain as the improved, reviewable source endpoint of the two-cycle exercise. Broadleaf color/relief and geometry are substantially better; the complete requested vegetation quality is not achieved because moss still fails to read as moss. This is a candid human-review package, not artistic acceptance or production admission. No further art cycle is prescribed here.

Read vegetation-brief.md and compared with the previously inspected VEG-001 reference and candidate1. Inspected actual candidate2 close, close-opposing, close-unlit, close-gray, close-geometry, overview, walking, grass-detail, moss-detail, moss-pocket-2 and moss-pocket-3 PNGs. Also inspected transfer/native.png and transfer/reimport.png and read its receipt.

Broadleaf leaves now spread into a low, smoothly curved rosette instead of the earlier upright folded paddles. Rounded contours, restrained green variation, scattered warm spots and branching veins improve the identity. Opposing light retains that identity with plausible moving shadows. The separate pure-geometry control shows gentle cups; the gray relief control adds fine veins without suggesting oversized geometric ribs. The remaining shape is a simple, regular four-leaf arrangement with similar clean contours and a sparse central stem junction. It does not yet match the reference's irregular crown, leaf proportions and natural asymmetry, but is visibly more credible and worth retaining.

Seedlings provide a distinguishable smaller paired-leaf stage. Grass now has uneven lengths and outward bends, a useful improvement over straight spikes; close inspection still reveals angular bends and flat ribbons around a tightly gathered base. Walking shows more local growth-form variety. Overview remains dominated by the repeated broadleaf silhouette, with smaller forms difficult to distinguish at that scale. Sparse coverage and bare joints are appropriate; this is not a request for blanket density.

Moss is the concrete failed criterion. The unobstructed pocket-2 and pocket-3 views show long, smooth green strips with pinched lobes, clean boundaries and fine parallel texture. They read as laid-down ribbons rather than a low irregular mat of tiny growth. The earlier green-pebble form is gone, but neither the main detail nor these unobstructed captures establishes convincing moss. Reported near-soil placement does not resolve that visible identity failure; no claim of stone burial is needed.

The representative broadleaf transfer visibly retains its silhouette, muted leaf color, spots and fine vein response in the supplied matched view. No material discontinuity or conspicuous new artifact is apparent in that pair. Conditional pass for this limited Blender export/reimport screen only. The receipt reports 1,016 triangles, two materials, three textures and 2,444,100 bytes; those metrics are not visual acceptance. Native subsurface response may not transfer to standard glTF, and this single pair does not prove opposing-light or engine behavior. Grass, seedlings and moss are not validated by this representative weed transfer. The external map correction was reported by the author; the pair supports current visible color retention, not an independent encoding audit.

Accepted paving material/light/contact are outside this review. Existing paving repetition and long seams remain separate layout findings. Source texture-class exceptions, ground production-budget excess, full QA, rights clearance and runtime validation remain open independently of this native visual improvement. Human acceptance remains open, with moss failure and simplified vegetation silhouettes explicitly disclosed.
