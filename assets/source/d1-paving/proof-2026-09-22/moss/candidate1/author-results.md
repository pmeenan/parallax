# First explicit moss candidate — review pending

The four rejected smooth cushion meshes are replaced by four small colonies containing 1,434 explicit leafy shoots. Each shoot has a tapered triangular stem and seven individually cupped leaflets. Heights above actual soil reach 4.48–4.75 mm; stem roots enter soil by approximately 0.12 mm. There is no green backing sheet, mound or pellet geometry. Olive/deep-green variation is original linear vertex color, with darker stem bases and modestly lighter leaflet tips. Seed 922801 reproduces construction.

The batch116 VEG001 courtyard reference governs restrained joint placement and the surrounding planting context. It does not depict a moss species; shoot morphology is an original authored extension, not a claimed reproduction of that image. Original accepted leaf-color/candidate2 is the exact input and is not changed.

All nonmoss mesh positions, faces, UVs, color attributes, transforms and material assignments are hashed before and after. Existing nonmoss material node signatures are unchanged. Soil support uses the actual adaptive-earth BVH. Every accepted shoot vertex avoids conservative stone XY footprints; above-ground leaflets are checked against actual soil height. This is a focused support/clearance check, not a full triangle collision solver. The recipe chooses slightly wider nearby joint pockets, but centers still have only approximately 4–7 mm conservative stone clearance. This restricts colony silhouette and may remain visually limiting.

The scene contains 681,469 triangles, below the 750,000 source-study limit. Moss contributes 48,756; total plants are 54,665, exceeding the 4,000 production target. This explicit microgeometry is retained only to evaluate shape, and no production budget is changed. Macro leaflets have four triangles each and may appear angular. At walking distance the moss occupies a small, quiet fraction of the joint. Neither production performance nor artistic acceptance is claimed.

Six native captures show full material, opposing light, unlit moss color, gray geometry, walking distance and overview. The first macro captures inherited a 0.1 m camera near plane that clipped foreground geometry. capture-repair.py changes that setting to 0.001 m for close captures only; the saved source retains the original camera. Temporary diagnostic materials are recreated after load. No depth-of-field blur is used. Surrounding stone and soil softness at extreme macro is inherited and untouched.

Rights review remains false, source-only. Both independent screens and lead review are required before the second and final art cycle. Representative export/reimport is deferred until the native candidate is judged viable. No library, engine, compression or LOD validation has been performed.
