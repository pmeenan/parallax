# Layout-study candidate1: independent first-pass theme screen

**Outcome:** retain as a meaningful broken-joint improvement over patch candidate2, but do not accept it as KIT-001 layout fit. This is a source-layout diagnostic; the plants shown are provisional and this screen does not assess the approved leaf color or moss, whole-patch acceptance, QA, or runtime readiness.

Inspected the actual `top-packing`, `sunny-oblique`, `opposing-light`, `overcast`, `walking`, and `plant-contact` PNGs against patch candidate2's top, sunny and walking views and the approved KIT-001 batch103 assembly image. The top view now has a more useful mix of short rectangles, squares and turned stones. Joint ends interrupt several of the old continuous vertical lanes; the oblique and walking views likewise read less like parallel strips. The receipt reports 111 stones, five grid proportions and a longest seam of 16/28 cells versus patch candidate2's 28/28. That metric supports the visible direction of improvement but is not an artistic verdict.

The remaining joint rhythm is still too mechanically registered for the reference. In top view, long horizontal alignments recur across the middle and lower field, while groups of upright narrow stones form local channels. Oblique and walking views expose a regular small-paver grid; KIT-001 has broader, less uniform slab groupings with occasional small infill and irregular outlines. A 16-cell seam is still about 2.3 m on this 4 m patch. Some aligned seams are present in KIT-001, but they do not organize the whole surface as strongly as these do.

Material repetition is equally visible. All lighting views retain the intended limestone response, yet the same pale mineral islands and dark mottling recur at similar scale across neighbors, so the field has near-uniform visual activity. The layout rearrangement and rotations do not resolve the three-identity family limit; the receipt lists C on 73 of 111 stones. The plant-contact view confirms that joints remain legible at close range, but it does not change the layout or material conclusions.

For a second bounded layout cycle, prioritize fewer long aligned seams and larger, less uniformly proportioned slab groupings with rare small infill, then compare the same top and walking cameras. Material-family diversity remains a separate open criterion, not a reason to reject this layout improvement.
