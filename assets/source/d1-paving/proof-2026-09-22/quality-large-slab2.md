# Larger-slab candidate2 — independent final-cycle screen

I inspected the six native candidate2 views against candidate1 and KIT-001. This is the final bounded layout cycle and a construction review, not artistic acceptance.

**Layout:** Broad stones are distributed more evenly among the four quadrants than in candidate1; the upper half is no longer dominated by one isolated oversized block, and the top and sunny views read as a more consistent large-slab field. The strip clusters are reduced, but narrow runs remain through the lower center and right middle. Rectangular outlines and several connected joints still show the exact-cover grid beneath the composition. The receipt records 58 stones versus 61 before, with the same 14-cell longest seam. The improvement is in slab balance, not further seam interruption.

**Contact and artifacts:** The six views show a complete field and continuous earth. I see no clear floating slab, overlap, renewed bank-strip line, or lighting-specific tear. Some T-junctions still open into visible earth wedges, especially where broad slabs meet short infill. The plant-contact view shows a crown in the joint; deep leaf shadows conceal part of the root and adjacent stone shoulders, so still images cannot prove triangle-level clearance. The receipt reports no conservative AABB overlaps, 9.73 mm minimum AABB clearance, clear plant roots, and no low plant vertices within conservative stone footprints. Its 93,288 ground triangles are source-study geometry, not a production result.

**Material limit:** The same three mineral identities and pale island patterns repeat on multiple slabs. XY scaling spans 0.531–3.692, wider than candidate1, and the walking image makes the oversized mineral grain on central slabs conspicuous. This remains the main barrier to judging the larger geometry against KIT-001's varied, locally detailed stone faces. The new layout does not repair that material-scale mismatch.

`layout2.json`, the retained source files, and the recorded candidate outputs match the receipt hashes; layout and receipt agree on 58 stones and the 14-cell seam. These checks do not establish mesh-level contact or full QA. The scene still uses provisional plants and lacks accepted leaf-color/moss integration. It is a useful large-slab proportion diagnostic; final material, vegetation transfer, engine import, cost qualification, and human visual judgment remain separate.
