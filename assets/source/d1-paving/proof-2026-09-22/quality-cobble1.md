- Top packing has more interruptions and T-junctions than either older patch.
  The receipt's longest planning seam of 10 cells versus layout1's 16 is consistent
  with the visible improvement. Sunny/reverse still read as a coherent laid field
  of smaller rectangles and squares, without substituting broad slabs.
- Cream/warm-gray mineral identity, fine grain scale and worn edges remain in the
  earlier smaller-stone direction. Opposing light changes the exposed-side shadows
  plausibly; overcast keeps the stones and earthy joints readable. Gray/unlit do
  not reveal a new layout-specific material or geometry discontinuity.
- Plant-contact preserves the accepted muted green, subtle mottling, fine veins
  and curved leaf surfaces. Stems visibly emerge from joints; there is no obvious
  new floating crown or leaf slicing through a stone. Sparse placement leaves
  walking faces mostly clear. I am not reopening the accepted leaf design.
- Moss-contact retains the dense low green shoot character and fits within the
  soil pocket. Several isolated shoots are more exposed than in the original
  partially occluded close-up, but they do not establish a floating or unsupported
  integration defect. The receipt records rigid relative shoot preservation,
  positive leaf/soil clearance and zero projected stone-vertex conflicts; those
  are limited support checks, not full triangle-collision proof.
- Native and reimported representative moss look equivalent at the shown scale,
  with no conspicuous lost leaves, color change or altered silhouette. The transfer
  receipt reports mean loaded-pixel difference `9.58e-5`, 13,260 triangles and
  592,504 bytes. The flat preview floor is a transfer backdrop, not installed-soil
  contact evidence.

## Explicit remaining limits

The three mineral identities still repeat visibly. Walking shows recurring pale
diagonal bands, round islands and darker central fields, including the lower-right
run of long pieces. Rotating these changes orientation, not source identity. This
is inherited repetition, not a solved criterion or a new integration regression.

Registered lane-like joint runs remain visible in walking/grazing despite the
shorter maximum planning seam. Grazing also exposes dark recessed lengths and
thin cap-like edge profiles on the reused stones; close soil remains relatively
smooth between grit pieces. These limitations existed in the retained small-stone
contact/material setup. No new top intersection or discontinuous ground strip is
obvious here. A lower seam score alone does not establish final layout acceptance.

The receipt's 706,013 scene triangles fit the source-study ceiling, while 117,048
ground and 54,665 plant triangles exceed production targets. Retention therefore
means a reviewable source arrangement. Full QA, reduction/LODs, compression,
rights and installed-game behavior remain open. Preserve this candidate and state
its remaining repetition/seam limits rather than starting another material family
or silently treating the source study as production delivery.
