# Broad source family, cycle 2 — independent theme screen

2026-09-22. Independent visual screen against
[the bounded brief](broad-family-brief.md) and
[cycle 1 findings](theme-broad-family1.md). No source assets changed during review.

## Evidence inspected

Opened all seven actual `broad-family/candidate2/` captures: oblique,
opposing-light, gray, unlit, grazing, reverse and walking. Compared them with
the previously inspected seven `candidate1-corrected/` captures,
`restored-family/oblique.png`, KIT-001 Batch103 assembly and MAT-001 Batch108
cream limestone. The invalid initial precision preflight remains excluded.
The judgments below come from these images, not from seed values or the
reported shader construction.

## Verdict: provisional source for an early transfer test only

Candidate2 is materially better than candidate1. It restores warm cream/tan
mineral details against weathered gray, firmer broken mineral boundaries and
visible pores. It belongs substantially closer to the restored limestone
family than the previous cloudy gray-white surface. I support retaining it
and testing one representative budgeted export now.

This is not a theme pass for broad replication or a claim that the source
dependency is resolved. The four-stone sample remains more densely patterned
and less convincingly weathered than the references, and its walking view
introduces conspicuous flowing/smeared mineral shapes. Finish the planned
transfer test and record these limits at the allowance boundary; no third
native authoring cycle is implied by this review.

## Comparison and remaining defects

1. **Material identity improved; pattern hierarchy remains weak.** Oblique,
   reverse and opposing-light preserve a coherent cream/gray family. Unlike
   candidate1, the faces no longer read as blurred snowy clouds. But nearly
   every area of every stone carries similar light/dark activity. MAT-001
   has quieter gray areas interrupted by discrete cream mineral breaks and
   sparse pits; KIT-001 has noticeably different degrees of weathering among
   adjacent stones. Candidate2 still lacks that hierarchy and inter-stone
   variation in overall material character.
2. **Warped forms are visible at walking distance.** In `walking.png`, the
   upper-left stone has a conspicuous swept pale/dark formation just right
   of center, and the upper-right stone has several pale curved forms around
   gray centers across its middle. Similar streaked forms recur throughout
   the two foreground stones. They read more as flowing marbling than the
   irregular, relatively undistorted inclusions in MAT-001. The treatment
   breaks the old island outlines, but visible distortion is a remaining
   compromise, not evidence of independent geological features.
3. **No exact repeated large landmark confirmed in this sample.** I cannot
   confidently identify the same specific pale island in two stones after
   comparing walking, oblique and reverse. This is an improvement over the
   three obvious enlarged islands in the restored family. The recurring
   swept/horseshoe-like motif and similar overall density are still visible.
   A four-stone still does not prove absence of repetition in eight variants
   or a repeated patch, and differing seeds do not extend this observation.
4. **Geometry and contact remain limited evidence.** Gray still shows very
   smooth tops with little selective pit relief; most perceived mineral
   relief in the beauty images is therefore not established as geometry by
   this diagnostic. Grazing looks less like a continuous rolled sheet than
   candidate1, but short dark undercuts remain beneath the two foreground
   inner edges. These may be localized chips/side shading; the image alone
   does not prove floating or a correct substrate intersection. Contact
   should remain an open check when restored to the accepted earth sample.
5. **The sample is not the restored patch control.** The ground still reads
   smoother and less granular than `restored-family/oblique.png`. Vegetation,
   accepted patch lighting, layout and full stone-to-earth integration are
   absent from this sample. None receives acceptance from the new captures.

## Boundary and next evidence

An early transfer should establish whether the sharper warm mineral pattern,
restrained thickness and edge silhouette survive baking and fresh import at
the intended viewing distances. Transfer fidelity would validate that delivery
step, not cure the dense/swirl-like material character or prove native shape
quality. Keep candidate1 rejected and candidate2 explicitly provisional.

Human artistic acceptance, full family/patch assessment, eight-variant
completion, rights review, full asset QA, Babylon/WebGPU behavior and measured
game costs all remain open. If the current method is retained beyond this
bounded proof, record its unresolved material-hierarchy/distortion question
and obtain a new finite work allowance through the normal brief process;
do not silently promote this provisional screen into whole-patch acceptance.
