# Vegetation candidate2: independent final screen

Outcome: retain the improved vegetation study for human review and representative transfer evidence, but do not claim all vegetation/reference criteria passed. The two-cycle art boundary ends with moss readability and close naturalism unresolved.

Inspected all seven main native views (close, opposing, unlit, gray relief, pure geometry, overview, walking), grass-detail, moss-detail and all supplied leaf/moss maps. Compared with candidate1 and previously inspected VEG-001.

- Broadleaf contours are smoother and no longer the overlapping upright paddle pair. Fine secondary veins, restrained mottling and occasional warm spots are visible. Saved basecolor now visibly contains green color/veins, unlike candidate1's near-black file; native/file correction is visually consistent, pending transfer evidence.
- Distinct seedlings and bowed, uneven grass blades now accompany the broadleaf plant in close/walking context. This is a clear improvement in growth-form variety. Grass still has angular bends and flat ribbon faces at close scale.
- Broadleaf leaves remain very smooth and somewhat evenly arranged as four large panels. Pure geometry confirms limited fine shape articulation; gray relief adds modest veins without oversized ribs. The result remains simpler than VEG-001's irregular basal growth, natural leaf margins and textured surfaces. It is improved modeled vegetation, not reference-equivalent botanical realism.
- Moss-detail does not demonstrate a readable living cushion. The former green pebbles are gone, but the visible green element is a narrow smooth strip beside/below the broadleaf plant, partly occluded. Supplied moss maps show strong diagonal regular patterning; a map alone does not prove convincing moss in context. This criterion remains unproven rather than passed.
- Most joints remain bare. The focused walking view makes several growth forms legible together, while overview still reads primarily as a few isolated broadleaf marks. Sparse coverage is appropriate, but broad distribution of varied forms is only weakly evidenced at overview scale.

No additional artistic cycle is initiated. Present candidate2 with the explicit residuals above; representative export can test retention but cannot resolve native moss readability or confer human acceptance. Approved stones, lighting, soil contact and unrelated packing issues are outside this review. Source-only map allowances, production QA and runtime compatibility remain separate.
