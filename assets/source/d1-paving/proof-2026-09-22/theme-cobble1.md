# Smaller-stone patch, candidate 1 — independent theme screen

2026-09-22. Evaluated against [the continuation brief](cobble-brief.md).
The human's preference for the earlier smaller, cobblestone-like pieces
supersedes KIT-001's large-slab sizing. No acceptance of this new assembly is
inferred from that preference or from earlier leaf-color/moss acceptance.

## Evidence inspected

Opened all eleven actual `cobble-study/candidate1/` PNGs: sunny-oblique,
walking, top-packing, opposing-light, overcast, plant-contact, moss-contact,
gray, unlit, grazing and reverse. Compared against the previously inspected
sunny/walking views of `patch/candidate2` and `layout-study/candidate1`,
KIT-001 Batch103, MAT-001 Batch108, VEG-001 Batch116, and the accepted
`leaf-color/candidate2` and `moss/candidate1` close/walking captures.

## Verdict: retain for human layout and integration review

The patch restores the smaller-piece character requested by the human and
combines it with the accepted vegetation appearances. Local crosspieces and
staggered ends visibly interrupt some former long lanes; the overall field
still reads as intentionally laid limestone. The warm gray/cream family and
worn edges remain consistent across sunny, overcast and opposing-light views.
This is a supported improvement over the earlier assembly, with clear limits.

No concrete integration regression warrants another authoring cycle merely to
produce a second candidate. Retain this source and expose its remaining seams
and repeated stone patterns in the handoff. This verdict selects a reviewable
proposal; it does not close artistic, material-variation or production acceptance.

## Supported improvements

- **Scale and adjacency:** the compact rectangles/squares and occasional longer
  pieces make a coherent smaller-stone field. Top-packing shows varied local
  adjacency without a repeated checkerboard module. Walking view contains
  useful stone crossings where the older patches let the eye follow a lane
  farther. No return to broad slabs is required by this screen.
- **Plants:** the broadleaf color keeps the accepted healthy greens, restrained
  within-leaf variation and veins. Leaves emerge from joints and extend over
  stone edges. Sparse plants remain secondary to the paving; they are not
  used as a continuous border or camouflage for the layout.
- **Moss:** the accepted low, dense small colony is visible in a soil pocket.
  Its small scale and muted green keep it distinct from broadleaf plants and
  avoid a bright continuous moss grout line. There is no apparent wholesale
  change to the accepted leaf or moss appearance.

## Remaining visible limits

1. **Long joints are reduced, not eliminated.** Top-packing still permits long
   vertical traces just left of center and in the right side of the field.
   Walking and grazing views retain several lane-like dark joints. The supplied
   16-to-10-cell statistic is consistent with partial progress but cannot stand
   in for visual acceptance of these runs.
2. **The three mineral identities remain recognizable.** In top-packing, the
   small stones along the left edge repeatedly carry the same diagonal pale
   pattern. Walking view also repeats characteristic pale islands and similar
   light/dark balance among nearby faces. Changes of size or orientation do
   not establish new material sources. This is a layout/integration advance,
   not a resolved mineral-variation proof.
3. **The field remains relatively uniform in wear.** Most faces have comparable
   pattern density and tone. MAT-001/KIT-001 have stronger quiet/weathered areas
   and differences among adjacent stones. Gray also reveals smooth tops and
   similar softened margins; the material contributes much of the apparent
   surface complexity. These inherited limits are not grounds for a blind
   material rewrite in the remaining layout allowance.
4. **Very close vegetation/contact remains a source-study compromise.** The
   moss-contact image exposes the colony's crisp perimeter and a few isolated
   upright shoots, which read as tiny sprouts when separated from the mass.
   The accepted close hid more of that perimeter beside a stone. Plant-contact
   retains visibly simple petiole/leaf shapes and some faceted stone chips.
   These details do not dominate walking view and do not establish a new
   integration failure. Soil pockets and grit remain visible around the roots;
   geometry/support correctness belongs to the accompanying source checks.

## Disposition

Do not spend the optional second cycle on another arbitrary shuffle. Present
sunny-oblique and walking with the packing and contact evidence available,
and name the long-joint and three-pattern compromises explicitly. The human
can judge this coherent smaller-stone direction before another appearance
package is defined. Existing accepted leaf color and moss appearance remain
controls; the new layout and placement have not yet received that acceptance.

Source retention and prior representative transfers do not establish runtime
budgets, full LOD/compression, rights review, asset QA, library admission or
installed-game appearance. This independent screen changed only this report.
