# Four-metre patch final bounded screen

Verdict: retain candidate2 as the improved, human-reviewable native patch at the end of this two-cycle study. Reference fidelity remains incomplete; this is not human acceptance or production QA. No additional tuning cycle is prescribed.

Inspected all six actual candidate2 PNGs: sunny-oblique, opposing-light, overcast, walking, top-packing and plant-contact, against candidate1 and previously reopened KIT-001/VEG-001.

The overview and top packing now contain the complete patch. Continuous soil removes the conspicuous bank-strip borders and straight black line seen in candidate1's plant detail. The plant close-up has visible grit and a more coherent joint surface. It is still visually smoother and less clod-rich than VEG-001, but this does not reopen the accepted soil-edge decision.

Plants improve substantially: broad rounded leaves replace spear-like blades, mild cupping is visible, and midribs and branching stems read clearly. Their stems converge within the joint and the plant reads as seated there. Roots below the surface cannot be certified from these images. Close-view leaf silhouettes remain polygonal, midribs resemble thin applied lines, and secondary veins, surface variation and natural edge irregularity remain much simpler than VEG-001. The material/shape quality is adequate to judge placement at walking distance, not reference-complete hero foliage.

Packing has more interruptions and is readable as a mixed stone field, but long continuous seams still organize it into conspicuous lanes. Top-packing makes the remaining long runs clear; the reported 28-cell maximum is consistent with that visual concern, not independently counted here. The three recurring mineral identities and pale islands remain easy to recognize in walking views, producing more uniform busy coverage than KIT-001. Placement changes have not solved the available-variant limitation. Sunny/opposing/overcast views retain the accepted stone identity and response.

The production limit is separate from this visual verdict: the reported 115,904 ground triangles exceed the 8,192 substrate aim substantially. Reported 131 stones and 629,572 total triangles are within this study's placement/total limits, but do not cancel the ground excess. This is a source-only assembly; reduced ground delivery, existing encoded-size/LOD/compression gates, transfer/runtime verification and full QA remain outstanding.

Present candidate2 with these named residuals: long seams, recognizable three-identity repetition, simplified close foliage and excessive production ground geometry. Human review can now assess the larger composition honestly; the overall paving proof must not be labeled complete on this screen.
