# Layout-study candidate2: independent final theme screen

**Outcome:** the small continuous warp is a modest outline improvement over candidate1, without obvious collapsed joints or grossly distorted pavers in the inspected views. It does not make the field a KIT-001 layout match. Retain candidate2 as the final diagnostic of this bounded technique and stop the warp line here.

Inspected the actual `top-packing`, `top-packing-gray`, `sunny-oblique`, `opposing-light`, `overcast`, and `walking` PNGs against candidate1's matched views and the approved KIT-001 batch103 assembly. Gray top view makes the change clearest: edges gently bend and the formerly ruler-straight seams are less stark. The walking view also reads a little less manufactured at individual stone edges. The deformation remains small enough that rectangular slab identity and readable earth joints survive; I do not see a conspicuous pinched or folded paver in these captures. The opposing and overcast views preserve the same basic legibility under changed light.

The apparent course structure is almost unchanged. Several seams still align through many stones, and similar widths, right-angle junctions and repeated small rectangles keep the 4 m field regular beside KIT-001's broader, more varied slab groupings and occasional infill. The coordinated wave is itself a subtle procedural tell on the longer joints, especially in the gray top view. More of the same warp would risk visibly bent stones before it changes the underlying regularity. The receipt correctly leaves the 16-cell topology seam score unchanged; its reported approximately 7.9 mm post-warp separation is an analytic lower bound, not a visual-fit result.

The same pale mineral landmarks recur throughout sunny, opposing, overcast and walking views. Geometry warp has created no new stone identities or surface variation, so this remains a separate unmet criterion. The plants in this scene are provisional; approved leaf color and moss, integrated contact, QA, runtime behavior and human artistic acceptance are outside this screen. The next layout method should change adjacency and slab-size distribution rather than amplify global deformation, with material-family work evaluated separately.
