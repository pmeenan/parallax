# Final bounded leaf-color candidate

This second and final color cycle adds broad interveinal chlorophyll variation and partial faded margins to the existing vegetation. The 26 leaves retain differences between lighter new growth and darker mature growth; six receive partial edge fading and one receives an additional localized blemish. Most leaves remain healthy. Seed 922702 reproduces the authored color structure. No geometry, relief, roughness, stone, soil, grass, moss, or lighting art changes were made.

`receipt.json` records unchanged geometry, topology, UVs and transforms, unchanged normal/roughness dependency graphs, and unchanged nonleaf base-color graphs against vegetation/candidate2/source.blend. The input remains untouched. Five matched native views include close, opposing light, unlit leaf color, overview and walking distance. The editable source packs its image dependencies.

The saved base-color map is explicitly sRGB encoded and reloaded. A common 1.6 gain is included in the image while the per-leaf LeafAgeTint corner colors are divided by 1.6, retaining the intended native product with portable color values. The representative export explicitly selects LeafAgeTint as COLOR_0. This carries forward the first cycle's technical correction: an extra shader gain and exporting the intended tint as COLOR_1 had lost color in the initial transfer. That faulty mechanism is not used here.

The final representative transfer contains 1,016 triangles, two meshes, two materials and three textures in a 2,473,960-byte GLB. Fresh import versus native rendered pixel differences are mean 0.000170708, 99th percentile 0.003921568, and maximum 0.039215691 on a 0–1 scale. These are measured roundtrip differences, not a general visual acceptance threshold. Native 2.5% subsurface shading may not transfer through standard glTF. Native captures took 30.21 seconds; representative transfer and captures took 7.73 seconds. Timings and actual hashes are in the receipts.
