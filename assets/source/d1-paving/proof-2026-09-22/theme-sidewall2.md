# Sidewall2 independent theme screen

Outcome: reject as the reference-ready contact/profile solution. The two-cycle appearance criterion remains unmet; do not promote this deformation to a full patch on the strength of mesh validity.

Inspected the actual sidewall2 oblique, reverse-light, walking, overcast, joint-detail and diagnostic-gray PNGs. Compared with sidewall1, and reopened batch-104 KIT-002 v4, batch-108 MAT-001 v1 and original surface-study cycle11 oblique.

- The broad recession reduces the prominence of the old dark straight ledge. This supports the diagnosis that the band is dominated by exposed sidewall orientation, rather than a continuous void requiring higher soil.
- The replacement shoulder is too broad and continuous. Its wavy/ribbed rim reads as a deformed molded edge around a flat plateau, rather than irregular worn limestone planes. Gray diagnostic makes the smooth inclined belt and corrugations particularly clear.
- Pinched pleats and wedge-like corner notches are visible in ordinary oblique/walking views, especially the near corners and long member's near-right end. These are substantial silhouette regressions, not microscopic polish requests.
- Central top landmarks and substrate arrangement remain recognizable. This does not establish material fidelity or acceptance. No recommendation to raise soil or cover the edge with more aggregate follows from this screen.

Reference comparison also exposes an independent material concern: original cycle11 has stronger mineral relief, darker locally varied islands and warmer cream variation. The normalized current material reads pale, soft and flatter against both selected references. Its earlier conditional viability for family/transfer evidence must not be interpreted as a reference-quality pass. Because original and current images differ in geometry, scale and lighting, a matched unchanged-geometry material diagnostic is needed to isolate the cause; this screen alone does not justify blindly restoring all original shading.

Boundary: retain the earlier family-contact2 baseline and these experiments as evidence, rather than adopt sidewall2's distorted rim. Further artistic revision needs a separately bounded decision; this screen does not initiate another tuning cycle. Zero flipped/degenerate sampled triangles is a technical result, not evidence of natural wear. Human artistic acceptance, full-patch repetition and runtime validation remain open.
