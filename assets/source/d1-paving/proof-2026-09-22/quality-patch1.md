# Four-metre patch cycle 1 screen

Verdict: useful larger diagnostic, but fails the assembly/vegetation reference target. Use the one remaining bounded revision for assembly adaptation and plant quality. Preserve accepted stone material, lighting and soil-edge character.

Read `patch-brief.md`; inspected all six original candidate1 PNGs and reopened KIT-001 batch103 and VEG-001 batch116. Counts are separate from this visual assessment.

The stones remain recognizable limestone under sunny, opposing and overcast light. The larger field exposes repetition: familiar pale mineral islands recur frequently, with nearly uniform busy coverage and stone value. More decisively, top-packing and walking show uninterrupted parallel seams and strip-like courses, especially the vertical band right of center. KIT-001 has shorter interrupted courses, occasional infill and more varied visual groupings. Rotations alone do not conceal the recurring islands.

The new larger substrate adaptation is visibly unfinished. Walking shows straight bank-strip borders, while plant-contact has a sharp black straight line in the lower-right joint and broad smooth soil areas. This is an assembly seam/artifact issue, not a rejection of the human-approved small-sample contact character. That approved compact granular joint treatment needs to survive the new assembly.

Plants are sparse and do emerge from joints, with visible stems connecting to leaves. However, plant-contact reads as angular flat green blades on straight sticks. The large leaf has polygonal shoulders, a blunt cut-like tip and parallel banding; its narrow neighbor reads almost as a grass blade. The reference has cupped asymmetric broad leaves, readable midribs/branch veins and a clustered base seated in granular earth. The candidate's base is largely hidden behind the foreground stone, so this capture does not positively demonstrate a convincing crown-to-soil connection. No unsupported claim of disconnected mesh is made.

Priority for the single remaining revision:

1. Interrupt the longest seams with cross-oriented groups/infill; avoid adjacent placements repeating the same prominent island and orientation. Preserve mineral scale/material. Do not commission new surface generation for this cycle.
2. Remove exposed straight bank-strip borders and restore granular joint continuity around the new placement layout, including plant bases. Preserve the accepted edge profile.
3. Improve a small reusable plant set: rounded/tapered asymmetric broadleaf outlines, modest curvature, a readable midrib and crown/stem connection. Reduce the folded-card and linear-band appearance before adding fine decoration.

Frame the complete patch inside the overview/top capture as a cheap evidence correction; current overview corners and top/bottom packing boundaries are clipped.
