# Broken-joint layout candidate1 — independent first-pass screen

**Verdict:** a measurable and visible improvement over patch/candidate2, but the long-joint visual defect remains. Keep this as a reviewable first native cycle, not a final layout or whole-patch acceptance.

I inspected all six actual candidate1 PNGs at their recorded cameras: top-packing, sunny-oblique, opposing-light, overcast, walking, and plant-contact. The top view shows many more T-junctions and short stops than patch/candidate2. The receipt and `layout2.json` agree on 111 stones and a 16-cell longest internal seam, down from the prior patch's reported 28 cells. All six PNG SHA-256 values match the receipt. This geometric score supports the comparison; the visual judgment comes from the renders.

The improvement is incomplete. Several uninterrupted straight joints still run through much of the field, particularly around the central vertical band and across broad horizontal courses. The regular orthogonal grid and repeated narrow rectangular sizes still make the field read as organized brick lanes at overview and walking scale. KIT-001 has more varied slab silhouettes and less obvious modular cadence. The current three mineral identities also repeat recognizable pale islands and mottled landmarks across adjacent stones; the shorter seams do not cure this separate reference gap.

Packing and contact remain plausible in these stills: the field appears complete, joint widths are generally narrow and coherent, and I see no clear stone overlap, floating slab, or renewed black bank-strip line. The close contact view retains continuous earth and sparse grit. Strong leaf shadows obscure part of that joint, so the still alone cannot prove all under-plant contact or absence of small mesh intersections. Sunny, opposing, and overcast captures preserve a consistent stone material response; I see no new lighting-specific artifact attributable to the layout.

For the second bounded native cycle, prioritize disrupting the remaining long central and cross-field straight runs while checking the result again from the walking camera. Do not trade away the tight packing/contact just to lower a seam metric. Mineral repetition remains a separate source-family limitation. These captures use older provisional plants, so this screen does not evaluate approved leaf color or moss. No production cost, runtime QA, library admission, or overall artistic acceptance follows from this layout diagnostic.
