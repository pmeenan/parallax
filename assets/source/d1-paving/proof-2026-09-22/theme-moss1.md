# Moss candidate 1 — independent native screen

Read moss-brief.md and inspected all six actual corrected native captures: close, close-opposing, close-unlit, close-gray, walking and overview. Approved leaf color, lighting, soil and stone work are outside this assessment.

**Verdict: viable construction for a representative transfer test, with a remaining close-view moss-identity weakness.** This is a substantial improvement over the rejected ribbons: individual leafy shoots, overlapping depth and a broken colony silhouette now exist. At walking distance the low green patch reads plausibly as moss growing in a sheltered joint, with ample bare soil around it. It no longer looks like a smooth green strip or scattered green stones. Restrained green color and sparse placement fit the courtyard theme.

The close image still resembles a dense mass of miniature pointed broadleaf plants, or angular leaf confetti, more than a fine living moss cushion. Repeated flat diamond/spear leaflets are particularly evident at the upper-left fringe and in the gray control. The broad patch has fairly even interior density and a relatively abrupt edge despite the individual protruding shoots. Unlit confirms the silhouette and color rather than rescuing the anatomy; the opposing image puts much of the patch into shadow and is not evidence that this concern disappeared.

If the remaining bounded cycle is used after both screens, target finer/narrower slightly curved leaflets, small overlapping height/density clusters and a more gradual fringe with isolated sparse shoots. Preserve visible soil gaps and current restrained scale; increasing coverage or uniform density would worsen the concern. Do not return to ribbons or pebbles. No claim of exact botanical species matching is appropriate: VEG001 establishes sparse courtyard ecology, not a moss anatomy reference.

Overview cannot resolve moss anatomy and adds no close-quality clearance. Native viability is conditional; representative export/reimport has not yet been inspected.
