# Vegetation cycle 1 screen

Verdict: secondary veins and restrained color variation improve the leaf surface, but the representative vegetation fails the requested natural variety/geometry target. One bounded revision is warranted; accepted paving material, lighting, contact and layout are outside this screen.

Read `vegetation-brief.md`. Inspected close, close-opposing, close-unlit, close-gray, overview, walking, grass-detail and moss-detail actual PNGs, compared with the previously viewed VEG-001 reference. Gray retains normal relief; it is not a geometry-only diagnostic.

Broadleaf detail now has branching veins and subtle tonal variation without excessive surface noise. However, the tall paired leaves form an upright folded paddle; their polygonal edges and abrupt bends dominate over that useful texture. Several leaves overlap into sharp planar shapes. The reference's varied low rosette, rounded asymmetric leaves and gradual cups are more convincing. Long straight angular stems make the candidate look assembled from sticks and cards. Visible leaf/stem connections exist, but the base lacks a convincing clustered crown. Strong cast shadows move with opposing light; shadows alone are not evidence of incorrect lighting, though the stiff silhouettes make them conspicuous.

Grass is distinguishable in the dedicated view but reads as a few straight triangular spikes from a single rigid base. Moss reads as separate green faceted pebbles rather than a low irregular mat of fine growth. Smaller plants add scale variation, but overview/walking still read mainly as sparse repetitions of the same upright broadleaf type. Most joints remain bare, which is appropriate; variety needs clearer growth forms, not blanket extra density.

For the final cycle, prioritize gradual low/asymmetric broadleaf cups and rounded contours, shorter curved/tapered stems converging into a crown, and varied leaf inclinations instead of the tall folded pair. Retain the useful restrained vein/color work. Give grass blades a few gradual bends and uneven lengths/spread; make moss lower, smaller and partially merged into irregular protected patches rather than isolated green stones. A small seedling should have its own simple low paired-leaf silhouette. Keep clusters irregular and sparse. Avoid spending the remaining cycle on more vein noise while the silhouette is unresolved.

The reported external basecolor PNG encoding error requires correction and representative reimport verification; these native captures reportedly use the correct packed float image, so they do not certify that external map. Source-only texture-class excess and all production/runtime/human acceptance gates remain separate. Do not infer acceptance from the existence of all requested growth-form objects.
