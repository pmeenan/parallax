# Production candidate 1 — independent appearance-preservation screen

2026-09-22. Read [the production brief](../production-brief.md) and opened
the completed candidate's `sunny-oblique.png`, `walking.png`,
`plant-contact.png` and `moss-contact.png`. Compared these directly with the
previously inspected corresponding views of the human-approved
`cobble-study/candidate1`. This screen concerns preservation of that baseline;
it does not reopen its accepted art direction.

## Verdict: reject this reduction for appearance preservation

The distant stone field retains the approved smaller-piece scale, arrangement,
cream/gray mineral surfaces and general lighting. The vegetation reduction
does not preserve the approved appearance, and soil/contact also regresses.
Meeting the old geometry ceilings therefore does not make this a viable
replacement for the accepted source.

- **Leafy moss is lost.** In moss-contact, the dense low green colony has become
  widely separated thin L-shaped stems. Its leafy silhouettes, coverage and
  soft collective shadow are almost entirely absent. This is a categorical
  change of appearance, not merely reduced microdetail.
- **Leaf shapes are visibly damaged.** In plant-contact, the main broadleaf
  leaves have angular polygon boundaries and kinked vein flow. The smaller
  seedlings have conspicuous triangular/folded silhouettes in place of the
  accepted curved leaves. The green color family is broadly retained, but
  retained color does not preserve the botanical character when the outline
  and surface shape change this much.
- **Soil contact is weakened.** Close contact views lose much of the embedded
  grit visible in the accepted source. The soil pockets consequently read
  emptier and deeper around the unchanged stone edges. Sunny-oblique also
  exposes a straight rectangular substrate edge around the patch, absent
  from the accepted presentation. These observations do not alone prove a
  root gap; support geometry should be checked independently.

The walking and sunny views conceal much of the vegetation loss because the
plants are small in frame. They are insufficient to override the matched
contact evidence. No new mineral-pattern or stone-scale defect was apparent
in these four views.

## Bounded correction target

Use the final allowed cycle to preserve leafy moss coverage and individual
leaf silhouettes, the approved broadleaf/seedling curves, and the source's
recessed but filled soil contact. Keep stones, mineral material, placements
and accepted foliage colors fixed. Do not spend this cycle on unrelated
layout or artistic refinement. Inspect the same matched contact and walking
views before retaining the next candidate.

If preserving these appearances needs more geometry than the old class,
record the measured need and the unfulfilled class requirement explicitly.
This screen grants no class change, runtime qualification, QA admission or
export-fidelity pass. Only this report was changed by the reviewer.
