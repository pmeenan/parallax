# Production candidate1 — independent quality screen

Disposition: **reject as a reduction that preserves the human-approved look**.
The approved `cobble-study/candidate1` remains the baseline. This finding concerns
the production reduction, not a reopening of the accepted stone art or layout.

Read `production-brief.md`, `production/build.py` and candidate1's receipt. Actually
inspected candidate1's `walking.png`, `plant-contact.png`, `moss-contact.png` and
`grazing.png` against the previously inspected approved captures. These four views
establish the core failures; this is not an exhaustive lighting or export screen.

- **Moss loses its identity.** The dense leafy joint colony becomes sparse bent
  lines and isolated L-shaped silhouettes. Most visible leaf area and the low,
  continuous leafy coverage disappear. This directly fails the brief's requirement
  to preserve leafy silhouettes, even though walking distance conceals some loss.
- **Other plants lose their approved silhouettes.** In plant-contact, broadleaf
  outlines are conspicuously angular, with kinked veins at simplified bends.
  Seedlings become triangular planes or flat strips. The muted olive color broadly
  survives; retaining color does not compensate for the geometric change.
- **The joint contact becomes emptier.** Much of the visible grit is gone and earth
  reads smoother in plant-contact and grazing. Some dark exposed stone sides appear
  deeper. These images do not establish a new floating-root defect, but the contact
  appearance is not fully preserved.
- **Stones remain consistent.** The inspected mineral patterns, smaller scale and
  placement arrangement appear unchanged. No new stone-art demand follows from
  this screen.

The receipt reports 8,098 substrate triangles, 1,853 native plant triangles and
3,706 with every plant face duplicated. That last number is the builder's accounting,
not an independently inspected production export. Reported soil height deviation
is 5.86 mm maximum absolute and 2.82 mm at p99. Meeting these geometry counts does
not establish appearance fidelity, resource QA or runtime acceptance.

For the remaining bounded cycle, preserve the moss leaf silhouettes and colony
coverage, keep recognizable curved broadleaf and seedling outlines, and compare
grit/earth contact at the same cameras. Measure the resulting cost explicitly. A
higher-detail experiment can answer what preservation costs; this screen grants
no class-budget waiver or asset admission.
