# Delivery candidate1 independent quality screen

Screened on 2026-09-23 against the approved production/candidate3 appearance,
using the delivery brief and production-results.md. Actual candidate1 control/shared
walking, plant-contact, moss-contact and grazing PNG pairs were opened and inspected.
This is a transfer-fidelity screen, not a reassessment of the accepted source art.

**Disposition: reject candidate1's shared-material appearance pending correction.**

1. **Broad-leaf color is not preserved.** In walking and plant-contact, the
   control's varied green leaves become largely uniform dark gray-green. The
   contact view also loses the small warm color flecks. Veins and silhouettes
   remain, but retaining those does not preserve the approved leaf color.
2. **Pale grit becomes charcoal.** Both contact pairs show the same placed grit
   pieces changing from pale mineral accents to dark gray pieces. Walking and
   grazing also show the loss of pale joint accents. This is a material/color
   regression, not an objection to the approved grit shapes or distribution.
3. **Soil grain is lost.** In both contact views, the control's fine mottled soil
   becomes a broad, smooth brown surface. This changes the apparent material and
   makes the unchanged small plants and grit read more starkly against the floor.
   The moss-contact pair is especially clear evidence; the colony itself appears
   preserved while its surrounding ground loses texture.

No concrete new stone silhouette, placement or mineral-pattern regression is
visible in these four pairs. The moss retains its green leafy form, density and
placement in the inspected contact view. Grazing preserves the stone heights and
joint silhouettes. These observations are limited to rendered views; they do not
establish exact geometry preservation or export validity.

Correct the three demonstrated material-transfer regressions within the existing
allowance and compare the same views. Do not reopen accepted repetition, joint
layout, leaf geometry or close-up moss limitations. Fresh-import fidelity,
remaining lighting states, LOD behavior, compression and runtime costs are outside
this screen and still require their respective evidence.
