# Delivery candidate1 independent fidelity screen

Disposition: reject candidate1 for delivery fidelity. This is the independent
color/material/contact screen requested by the delivery brief, not artistic
acceptance or QA admission. Read the delivery brief and approved production result;
actually inspected all six control/shared pairs for walking, plant contact, moss
contact, sunny oblique, opposing light and overcast.

## Concrete regressions

- `candidate1/control-plant-contact.png` versus `shared-plant-contact.png`:
  the large four-leaf weed and two smaller seedlings change from varied green
  foliage to nearly uniform dark charcoal-gray. The approved mottled leaf color
  and subtle spots disappear; veins remain but read much more strongly against
  the flat dark surface. This is obvious at walking distance as well, and persists
  in sunny, opposing and overcast overview pairs.
- The same plant-contact pair and the moss-contact pair change the approved pale
  mineral grit to dark gray pieces. The silhouettes and locations remain visually
  matched, but the pale volumetric contact accents specifically restored and
  approved in production candidate3 are lost.
- Soil in both contact pairs loses its fine granular material response. The
  control's detailed sandy surface becomes smooth, blurred brown, most clearly
  across the large open region around the moss colony. This changes the contact
  reading even though geometry and cast-shadow placement appear retained.

## Preserved appearance and scope

The visible stone pattern, pale/dark mineral distribution, bevels, placements and
joint layout appear preserved across the inspected pairs. Moss retains green
color, individual leafy shoot silhouettes and the same colony coverage in its
close view. Plant roots and moss have no newly obvious floating gap in these
matched images. These visual observations do not establish exact geometry or
color-stream identity.

Correct the leaf and grit color transfer and recover the soil's fine material
detail before requesting a delivery handoff. Recheck these same matched close
views and walking view after the correction. No source art change is justified
by these transfer regressions. This screen did not inspect LODs, decoded resource
identity, runtime output or fresh-import pairs; those remain separate evidence.
