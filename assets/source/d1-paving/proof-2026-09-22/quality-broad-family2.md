## Changes and remaining blockers

- **Material identity recovered in part.** Cream/buff against warm gray and
  mineral/pore transitions now read as related to the restored source and MAT-001.
  The cool cloud coating is gone. However, the walking view is crowded with
  similarly sized pale islands and stretched, swirling streaks across virtually
  every face. Oblique and reverse views retain this even all-over activity.
  MAT-001 and KIT-001 have more connected calm worn fields and more localized
  damage. The remaining uniform visual noise is a reference-fidelity blocker,
  not a reason to label the family finished after its second cycle.
- **Scale is more plausible than enlarging the old mineral islands, but the
  material hierarchy remains weak.** Large source landmarks no longer fill entire
  slabs. Fine pitting is subordinate to broad dimensions. Gray still shows nearly
  flat interiors, while textured walking/grazing views imply much more craggy
  relief. The bake must preserve restrained depth and avoid amplifying those
  warped streaks into excessive normal relief. A receipt period alone does not
  establish physically calibrated appearance.
- **No specific duplicated landmark established.** I cannot point to one exact
  copied island, pit cluster or silhouette repeated between two candidate2 stones
  in these views. That is a useful improvement in this four-piece sample. Shared
  island size, coverage and marbled organization still create family-wide visual
  sameness. The captures do not prove installed-patch nonrepetition.
- **Contact remains unresolved.** The obvious cycle1 front-right stepped ledge is
  less pronounced, but grazing still exposes a dark horizontal recess beneath
  that top edge and a related undercut appearance near the front-left stone's
  inner corner. Thin shadow lengths remain around several exposed sides.
  The smooth soil channels contain no visible embedded grit or irregular shoulder
  overlap comparable with the approved contact control. Since no grit geometry
  was added and the patch was not rebuilt, this is an open context dependency,
  not evidence that approved contact has transferred to the new family. No top
  intersection is visible; images and AABB clearance cannot establish geometric
  contact correctness.
- **Relighting:** opposing light moves the edge shading to the expected sides.
  The color pattern remains present in unlit, as expected. I find no concrete
  contradictory directional highlight/shadow pair that establishes lighting
  contamination; the unlit image by itself does not justify another rewrite.

## Bounded outcome

Preserve candidate2 as a provisional source and use the representative transfer
only to answer source-to-bake fidelity at walking/grazing and opposite-light views.
Retain the noisiness, warped mineral organization and contact caveats in its result.
Do not enlarge placement or infer final family support from a successful reimport.
Reference fidelity and accepted joint/contact integration remain open at this
brief's allowance boundary; any further source correction needs its own recorded
bounded question. Full QA, rights, LOD/compression and engine proof remain separate.

## Representative A transfer addendum

Actual-viewed `broad-family/transfer/a/` high-source, high-source-opposing,
native-low, reimport and reimport-opposing, followed by all six matched walking
and grazing high/low/import captures. Read `verification.json` and
`matched-views.json`. **The matched views establish a source-to-bake fidelity
blocker. This corrects my earlier oblique-only assessment that the broad pattern
was closely preserved with mainly fine-edge smoothing.**

The silhouette and overall palette remain similar, but interior mineral landmarks
move and change organization. In the walking high image, the prominent pale angular
patch near image coordinates (715, 640) and the neighboring dark corridors do not
match the reduced/imported result; several upper-left and upper-middle groups also
differ. This is not explained by fine smoothing or silhouette changes. Grazing shows
different mineral placement on the exposed front side as well. The front-right rim
crease already exists in the high source and is not an import-only defect.

Reduced and imported versions remain visually indistinguishable at these capture
scales. Recorded mean loaded-pixel differences are about `8.22e-7` walking and
`1.36e-6` grazing, versus source-to-reduced `0.0500` and `0.0115` respectively.
Raster deltas include shadows/silhouettes and are not perceptual pass thresholds;
the changed interior landmarks are the direct visual evidence. The low/import
agreement places the observed divergence before final export/import, but images
alone do not establish the exact transform, coordinate or bake cause. The transfer
cannot currently support a claim that it preserves the selected source material.

The report records 3900 triangles, sampled source-distance p95 0.807 mm and maximum
2.628 mm, and 10,977,740 raw GLB bytes, exceeding the 8 MiB ceiling. Class width
eligibility also remains a blocker identified by the lead. Those geometry and
export/import checks do not cancel the visible source-to-bake mismatch. Walking
and grazing are now inspected, and their material fidelity is blocked pending
diagnosis. The source's pervasive marbling and unresolved installed soil contact
also remain open. No QA admission, family acceptance or engine claim is supported.
