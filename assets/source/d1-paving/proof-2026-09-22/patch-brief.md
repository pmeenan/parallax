# Four-metre paving assembly

The human approved the soil-to-stone edge and subsequently the moving-light result,
then explicitly requested the larger patch. Preserve the current restored mineral
material and accepted contact character. Current reduced A/B/C have conditional
independent transfer passes; use these rather than the older normalized exports.

Build one 4×4 m native source scene against KIT-001 batch103, KIT-002 batch104,
MAT-001/MAT-018 batch108 and VEG-001 batch116. Mixed rectangular sizes, short broken
courses and occasional infill; no repeated three-stone module. Soil stays recessed
and compact, with sparse embedded grit. Sparse asymmetric broadleaf plants have
readable leaf/stem/root connections in soil joints, not spiky grass or a green carpet.

Two art cycles maximum, 40 active / 60 elapsed minutes for this source package.
Inspect actual native captures with two independent reviewers and lead before any
revision. Capture sunny oblique, opposing light, overcast at the same oblique camera,
walking distance, top packing and plant/contact close detail. Save an editable scene,
source/placement provenance, physical dimensions, geometry counts, hashes and checks
for stone overlap and plant roots in joints. Keep original files and live Blender
untouched. Blender 5.2.1 isolated native processes are the authoring path.

Use the current <=4000-triangle stones, <=200 placements and <=750000 scene triangles
as the study's geometry limits. Aim for <=8192 substrate triangles and <=4000 plant
triangles; identify any source-only excess rather than claiming full QA. Existing
three-material/shared-texture, encoded-size, LOD, compression and admission gates
remain unchanged. Avoid silently stretching mineral texture scale to fit placements.

The study ends with a human-reviewable larger patch or named failed criteria.
Repetition may expose the need for more distinct variants (maximum eight under the
current class); do not commission new surface generation automatically. The small
C export mark remains known delivery correction work. This is not installed-game
verification, final packaging, rights clearance or a claim that the overall proof is done.

## First cycle disposition

Candidate1: 132 stones, 524974 total triangles, conservative AABB clearance >=10.019 mm,
ten plant roots in clear joints. Actual six-view lead and independent screens reject
the assembly: long straight seams, conspicuous repeated mineral landmarks, visible
soil-bank mesh lines, flat pointed leaves and clipped framing. These are newly exposed
assembly defects, not a reopening of the accepted small-sample contact or lighting.

Use the second and final cycle for interrupted courses and nonadjacent landmark
placement, a continuous soil surface with the approved granular contact character,
rounded cupped leaves with readable midribs/crowns, and complete framing. Preserve
stone materials. A source-only ground/plant class excess may be recorded where
necessary to retain appearance, within the 750000 scene study ceiling; it must not
be called production QA or silently change the class budget. No new stone image
generation is included. If three mineral identities remain too repetitive, report
that family-expansion requirement rather than hiding it through color changes.

## Final disposition

Both cycles are complete. [Candidate2 results](patch-results.md) retain the native
4 m patch for human review with long joints, repeated mineral identities and
simplified foliage still unresolved. Soil strip lines and framing were corrected.
Ground geometry is explicitly source-only over target. No full acceptance, runtime
