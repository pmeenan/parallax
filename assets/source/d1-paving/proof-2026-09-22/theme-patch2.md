# Four-metre patch candidate2: independent final theme screen

Outcome: retain candidate2 as the improved native larger-patch study and present it for human review with named residuals. It is not a complete KIT-001/VEG-001 reference-fit pass or production-ready package. The two-cycle boundary ends here.

Inspected all six actual candidate2 PNGs: sunny-oblique, opposing-light, overcast, walking, top-packing and plant-contact. Compared with candidate1 and the previously opened KIT-001/VEG-001 references.

Improvements are concrete: the entire patch is framed; conspicuous bank-strip construction lines are absent from the shown joint detail and walking view; plants have broader rounded leaves, visible midribs, more readable branching petioles and a fuller basal form. Sparse asymmetric planting and the restored limestone identity remain. Lighting and the approved local soil-edge character are not reopened.

Remaining visual criteria:

- Long parallel joint runs still organize the field into lanes. Some local course interruptions help, but top-packing and walking continue to read more regular than KIT-001's short courses and varied infill. The reported maximum internal run of 28 cells is consistent with this visible residual, not proof that it is solved.
- Three mineral identities remain recognizable as repeated stamps. Rotation and mixed shapes avoid a literal repeated three-stone module, but several cream islands recur in neighboring placements and the overall field has similar contrast everywhere. This is the main family-diversity limitation exposed by the larger study.
- Plant silhouette is substantially closer to VEG-001, yet plant-contact still shows angular leaf perimeters, mostly smooth green faces and simple thin midrib lines. The leaves read as simplified modeled foliage, not the reference's organically varied veined surfaces. Suitable for judging placement/composition, not a claim of close-detail botanical fidelity.
- Soil is continuous and has visible embedded grit, but the narrow channels are visually spare compared with the selected assembly/plant references. This records reference fit only; it does not revoke the human's local contact approval.

Production accounting is separate from native visual judgment: reported ground geometry is 115,904 triangles against the 8,192 substrate target, despite the reported 629,572 total remaining within the 750,000 study ceiling. The ground is explicitly source-only excess. Reported non-overlap and conservative plant checks do not establish final art quality. Packaging/size, ground production representation, runtime behavior and human artistic acceptance remain open.

No further tuning or generation is recommended within this bounded run. Candidate2 is a clearer, honest review artifact; remaining packing regularity, three-identity repetition and simplified foliage prevent calling the whole proof complete.
