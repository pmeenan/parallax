## Required comparisons and must-fix criteria

Walking-height sunny/overcast on identical scene/camera/materials, oblique assembly,
top packing view and grazing close detail. Source evidence must expose varied worn
rectangular shapes, broken course lengths, small infill, broad calm tops with localized
wear, subdued cream/buff/gray variation, embedded shoulders, textured recessed soil,
and sparse rooted broad-leaf plants. Avoid manufactured uniform bevels, identical
shape stamps, large bare flat channels, spikes, floating plants and pasted shadows.
Metric footprint/stone heights and topology/material counts recorded. Do not claim
4m scale or runtime budget proof from visual impression alone.

Root and two independent Astra-low reviewers inspect actual renders against the
selected originals before cycle2. Human acceptance follows screening; a failed
source candidate is not exported/admitted or used to lower the selected target.
Source renders cannot establish runtime photometry, temporal stability, collision,
rights clearance or material-map calibration. No game/world placement change here.

## Cycle 1 — rejected by both screens and lead inspection

The [retained native source and captures](proof-2026-09-20/cycle1/receipt.md)
show 88 closed stones and 179,300 stone triangles. Matched walking daylight/overcast
captures preserve geometry and camera. These source measurements do not establish
export-budget compliance: mesh sharing, substrate density and material count still
require production conversion before QA.

Both independent Astra-low reviewers inspected all five captures against selected
103/104/108/116. Lead inspection agrees: smooth pale slab faces, broad flat brown
joints, soil intersections through stone tops, manufactured shoulders, long straight
packing seams and angular leaf fans fail the selected appearance. The top diagnostic
also clips the patch. Cycle 2 addresses those specific failures; no visual acceptance,
export or library admission follows from cycle 1. Original source and render bytes
are retained with hashes; historical generation scripts remain unchanged.

## Cycle 2 — visual target still unmet; initial proof closed

Lead actual-image inspection of all five native captures rejects the second cycle.
The [native receipt and source](proof-2026-09-20/cycle2/receipt.md) retain the failed
candidate, authoring snapshot, measurements and original images.
Both independent Astra-low reviewers also inspected all five final captures and
returned FAIL. Quality and theme screens agree on the camouflage mineral pattern,
rounded slab shoulders, flat soil with sprinkled gravel, stylized plant crowns and
long rectangular packing seams. Neither recommends export or assembly expansion.
Soil islands on tops are removed in the inspected views, joint aggregate is visible,
leaves have curvature, and the top diagnostic now includes the full patch. However,
the mineral shader produces repeated high-contrast camouflage-like gray blotches.
Smooth rounded shoulders and broad calm-but-featureless faces still read as cast
slabs. Grit remains visibly faceted and plants look like simplified smooth rosettes.
These are material and construction failures against the selected references.
The numeric contact audit still finds three sampled soil intersections out of 79,855
face-center probes, up to 1.314 mm; visual improvement is not collision clearance.

The source contains 93 closed stones and 188,700 stone triangles, 259,200 substrate
triangles, 11,000 grit instances and 18 plant crowns. These are source-study counts,
not QA compliance. Both render cycles are exhausted; no candidate is accepted,
exported, admitted or integrated. The required paving outcome remains open.

**Disposition:** defer further tuning of this full-assembly approach. Reopen with a
separate bounded study of one convincing worn stone and its immediate soil joint,
matching 104/108 at close and walking scale, before replicating its material across
the assembly. Preserve the useful soil/contact correction and matched capture setup;
do not preserve the failed camouflage pattern as an artistic baseline. This is a
proposed next production package, not authorization for an unbounded retry loop.
