# Cycle 2 — geometry only

Cycle 1 root and both independent screens failed before this revision began.
Cycle 1 scene, source and images remain intact. New build/capture scripts create and
save a separate cycle 2 scene through live Blender MCP.

Changes: moved the quarry shoulder away from the middle of the stone to remove its
continuous diamond-section band; replaced 13 isolated large cuts with 16 clustered
shallow flake cuts; widened restrained weathering; changed isolated top depressions
to shallow broad wear with smaller subordinate losses; added side fracture relief.
All relief remains actual mesh geometry with one plain-gray material, no texture maps,
and no procedural shader camouflage.

All five camera transforms and lenses are identical to cycle 1. Lighting is deliberately
changed for legibility: area key 170 W to 18 W and diameter 0.70 m to 0.55 m. The extra
`cycle1-under-cycle2-light.png` renders unchanged cycle 1 geometry with this new key;
it separates geometry changes from lighting changes. Its original source is unchanged.

Corrected build completed in approximately 5.6 seconds. Source is closed manifold,
824,912 triangles, 396.158 × 299.308 × 79.713 mm. The plain support sits at the lowest
mesh point, Z 1.113 mm. These are source checks, not runtime QA or export acceptance.

Author actually inspected all five final captures. The middle band and isolated pits
are improved, but the side relief appears too uniformly stippled and the top-to-side
fracture structure remains too simple. **Author does not recommend artistic acceptance
of this master as the selected limestone construction.** Root and independent screens
make their own adjudication. Both initial cycles are now consumed; no further artistic
mutation has been made. Material production, soil, vegetation, export and engine work
have not started.
