# Independent final theme screen

2026-09-21. Inspected actual native `family-contact1/oblique.png`,
`reverse-light.png`, `walking.png`, `overcast.png`, `joint-detail.png`, and all
three `portable-family-a/b/c/reimport.png` images. Compared with the previously
inspected family candidate2, contact-cycle3, KIT-002 batch104 v4 and MAT-001/MAT-018
batch108 references. This is a visual evidence screen, not source-save validation.

**Verdict: viable for human review of a small provisional sample; reference contact
fidelity remains incomplete.** The three mineral patterns and silhouettes remain
distinct, with no return of the rejected flowing stripes or obvious repeated central
landmark. Cream/gray stone and warm compacted earth form a coherent family at walking
distance. Portable reimports preserve those identities and thickness without a gross
material-transfer failure in the inspected views.

Compared with contact-cycle3, the combined sample has more visibly buried sidewalls
and less floating-slab appearance. The joints contain small grit and uneven earth, but
still fall short of MAT-018's locally dense embedded aggregate. The left stone retains
a long dark underside strip in joint-detail. Soil intersects some edges so abruptly
that their outlines look clipped or bitten away, particularly the front stone's
right edge and the long stone's left end. This is preferable to free-floating slabs
but does not establish natural contact throughout.

The combined stone tops also read softer and flatter than the isolated family2
presentation, especially in joint-detail and overcast. Mineral identity survives;
the selected reference's crisp mineral boundaries, small cavities and uneven edge
fractures are not fully reproduced. The portable isolated views retain readable
volume, so this screen does not attribute the combined softness to export alone.

For the next authorized refinement, prioritize a matched close-view check of stone
detail, localized tiny aggregate in joints, and irregular soil buildup against lower
sidewalls without clipping the top-edge silhouette. Do not globally densify open
ground or return to the rejected loose gravel bed.

Human artistic acceptance, full 4 x 4 m repetition testing, clean reflectance
qualification, source recovery verification, rights review, QA/library admission and
installed Babylon/WebGPU inspection remain open. No asset or scene was edited by
